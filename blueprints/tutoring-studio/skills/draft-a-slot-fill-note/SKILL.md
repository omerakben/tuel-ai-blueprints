# Slot-fill notes

An opening appeared. Claude drafts short notes to the families who said they wanted more sessions, offered first come, first served. You send them.

## When to run this

- A cancellation just opened a time you'd rather not leave empty.
- A family paused for the season and their regular slot is free.
- The weekly slot-fill check or the weekly schedule hour turned up openings worth offering.

## What Claude reads first

- business/clients.md — families whose notes say they asked for more sessions, who to write to, and anyone who asked not to be messaged.
- business/staff.md — whose opening it is and what subjects they cover, so the note only goes to families it fits.
- business/policies.md — any limits you've set on reaching out.
- business/brand.md — your voice, so each note sounds like you.
- templates/reminder-text.md — the shape each note starts from.
- The opening itself, from you: the day, the time, and the subject it suits. Claude never decides on its own that a slot is open.

## Steps

1. Name the opening: day, time, which tutor, and what subject it fits.
2. Claude lists the families from clients.md whose notes say they wanted more sessions and whose subject matches. A family with no such note stays off the list, no matter how good the fit looks.
3. Claude shows you the list before writing a word. Cross off anyone you like; Claude waits for your go-ahead.
4. Claude drafts one short note per approved family from templates/reminder-text.md — addressed to the parent or guardian, mentioning the student's subject, offering the time as "first to reply", and carrying an easy way to say "no more notes like this, thanks".
5. Claude shows you the whole batch. You edit or cut any note, then approve the keepers by name. You send them from your own phone or email — Claude never sends.
6. Claude saves the batch to reports/ so you can tick off replies as they come in.
7. When someone takes the slot, tell Claude — it drafts a quick "it's yours" confirmation for you to send and, with your yes, a kind "that one's gone" note for anyone else who answered.
8. If a family's answer changes what they want — no more notes, or a standing wish for Saturdays — Claude proposes the clients.md line and writes it only on your yes.

## What you get

One file saved as reports/slot-fill-YYYY-MM-DD.md: the opening, who was offered it and why, every approved note word for word, and a line under each to mark sent and answered.

## Never

- Never draft for a family that never asked for more sessions, or for anyone who asked for space. No cold notes.
- Never offer an opening you didn't name, and never invent one to fill a quiet week.
- Never promise the slot to more than one family without saying so — every note says "first to reply".
- Never write to the student; notes go to the parent, the guardian, or an adult student.
- Never mention why the slot opened up — another family's cancellation is their business, not a selling point.
- Never send a note, and never set one up to go out later. Drafts live in the report; your phone does the sending.

Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
