# Your AI adoption map

This map helps a tutoring studio add Claude work at a pace the owner can trust. Maturity belongs to each routine, not to the whole studio. You may stay at any stage with any routine for as long as you want.

No stage authorizes Claude to send, book or cancel a session, promise an outcome, change a schedule, charge or refund, judge a student, delete, or change an outside system. Use Manual mode for every new or changed routine until you review it again and approve its exact sources, output, and limits.

Only the owner may advance, pause, move back, resume, or retire a routine.

## Step 0. Readiness

Name the owner, choose one repeated admin job, and identify the current parent inquiry, approved session list, tutor notes, contact preference, or invoice list Claude may read. Define the local draft you want and what should stop the work. Start with one inquiry reply or factual session recap. Stop if consent, recipient, subject, rate, availability, notes, student record information, or responsible owner are unclear.

## 1. Assisted

Work beside Claude on one parent inquiry reply using `skills/draft-an-inquiry-reply/`, or one recap using `skills/draft-a-factual-session-recap/`.

- Provide the real inquiry or the tutor's current notes.
- Check every subject, session fact, rate, recipient, date, and promise.
- Review the draft and receipt before you contact or book anyone yourself.

Move only this routine forward when you have seen enough accurate work to trust its pattern.

## 2. Repeatable

Run a small manual batch of inquiry replies, factual recaps, session-option drafts, invoice reminders, or slot-fill notes.

- Keep each student, family, and session separate.
- Let missing consent, recipient, notes, or availability block only that item.
- Review the final drafts and owner decisions together.

## 3. Supervised operations

Turn one proven reports-only routine into a schedule. Start with the morning brief, end-of-day close, or slot-fill review.

Confirm the sources, run time, expected report, and pause conditions first. Every message, booking, schedule change, charge, refund, and promise remains your action.

## 4. Intent-led Business OS

Ask Claude to show which inquiries, recaps, open slots, invoice drafts, or scheduling gaps need your attention. Review one exception brief with the evidence and next decision.

Claude may surface missing recaps, unanswered inquiries, open slots, overdue invoice drafts, or work waiting for review. It never judges ability, predicts outcomes, handles school records, books, charges, or contacts anyone.

## Safe next uses

- Draft session options from confirmed tutor availability.
- Prepare slot-fill notes for owner-selected families who asked for more sessions.
- Summarize the weekly schedule and flag missing recaps or inputs.

## Stop or defer

- Pause if consent, recipient, session notes, rates, availability, or contact preferences are stale or disputed.
- Defer educational or ability judgment, predictions, school records, bookings, schedule changes, charges, refunds, customer promises, access changes, and deletion.
- Return any routine to an earlier stage whenever you want more hands-on review.
