# What to collect, in order

## Control choices

- Name the person who approves decisions and outside actions.
- Confirm the local time zone and usual working days.
- List which files and connected tools Claude may read for routine work.
- Choose how long temporary exports in `inbox/` should be kept. Claude may flag old files but never deletes them.
- Name the accountant, lawyer, technician, or other qualified person who reviews work outside Claude's lane, when relevant.
- Choose one repeated job for the first Assisted routine. Record how it works today, its approved sources, a useful result, and a stop signal.
- Review saved evidence before advancing a routine. A stage change never authorizes sending, posting, paying, filing, signing, deciding, or deleting.
<!-- TUEL:CONTROL-CHECKLIST:END -->

Claude works through this over time — never as a form, always as conversation. Tier 1 is enough for the first win. Everything else can wait.

## Tier 1 — needed for the first win

- Studio name
- What gets tutored (subjects, test prep, ages or levels) and the feel of the sessions in three words
- Days and rough hours when sessions happen
- Where sessions happen (the studio, the student's home, online, a mix)
- Session types with rate and length
- Who tutors, their subjects, which days — just the owner is fine
- How new families reach the studio (calls, texts, email, a form)
- Who the studio writes to about a student — parent or guardian, plus any adult students

## Tier 2 — makes it good

- The full rate list (photo, file, or pasted — any shape is fine)
- House policies: cancellations, makeup sessions, late arrivals, how families pay, session packs if any
- The regular families: parent or guardian name, student's first name, subject, usual session rhythm, and the family's goals in their own words — never grades, scores, or anything from school records
- Materials: workbooks, practice sets, printing — where they come from and when to restock
- Logo and a few photos the owner likes, dropped into `assets/`
- The studio's look and voice: three words, colors, phrases the owner loves, phrases to avoid
- Where reviews land (Google, Yelp) and whether the owner wants help replying

## Tier 3 — nice to have

- Busy and slow stretches as the owner sees them (exam season, summer, back-to-school)
- The studio's story — how it started, what it's known for
- How each tutor takes their pay (hourly, per session, a split), only if the owner wants the end-of-day close to track it
- Families who have asked for more sessions, so slot-fill notes have somewhere to start
- Anything with a renewal date the owner wants nudges about (insurance, certifications, background checks)
