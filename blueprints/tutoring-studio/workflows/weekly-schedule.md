# Weekly schedule hour

One hour a week to lay out the week ahead — sessions, openings, recaps owed, invoices to nudge. Claude preps every draft, and you decide what goes out.

## When

Once a week, at a time you pick. Many owners use Sunday evening or the quiet stretch before the after-school rush. Say "let's do the schedule hour" and Claude starts.

## Steps

1. Look back. Claude reads last week's schedule-hour report in reports/ and checks what got sent and what's still waiting. If there is no record yet, Claude says so and starts fresh.
2. Lay out the week. You paste the week's session list or read it out — Claude cannot see your calendar on its own. Claude maps it plainly: who's coming, which days are full, where the openings sit. Anything that looks off — a family missing from their usual slot — becomes a question to you, never an assumption.
3. Round up the loose ends. From reports/ and what you say, Claude lists the recap notes still owed, the invoices still open that you've named, and the families waiting on an answer from you.
4. Offer the openings. For openings you want to fill, Claude runs the slot-fill playbook: families from business/clients.md who asked for more sessions, list shown to you first, one draft per family you approve.
5. Draft the rest. Recaps owed go through the recap playbook from the tutor's notes; reminders for invoices you name go through the invoice-reminder playbook. Everything is a draft.
6. Approve the batch. Claude lays it all out in one place — every note, recap, and reminder. You go item by item: yes, no, or change it. Changed items come back for another look. An item you skip is a no. Only items with your fresh yes make the plan.
7. Make the week plan. Claude ends with a short plan: each approved item, the day you'll send it, and who it goes to. You do the sending. The plan and every approved draft are saved to reports/.

## What you get

- The week at a glance: sessions, openings, and loose ends on one page.
- Every approved draft — slot-fill notes, recaps, reminders — ready to copy and send.
- A one-page plan saved as reports/weekly-schedule-[date].md, with the approved drafts underneath it.

## Never

- Claude never sends, books, moves, or cancels anything. Not one word leaves this folder without you.
- Claude never invents an opening, a rate, an amount owed, or a session that isn't in your files, your reports, or what you just said. It asks.
- A yes covers that exact item, that week. Last week's yes approves nothing this week, and staying quiet on an item is a no.
- No judgments of any student's ability anywhere in the plan or its drafts — facts about sessions, nothing more.
- Every draft is written for a parent, a guardian, or an adult student — never for a minor.
- Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
