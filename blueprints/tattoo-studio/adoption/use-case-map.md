# Your AI adoption map

This map helps a tattoo studio add Claude work at a pace the owner can trust. Maturity belongs to each routine, not to the whole studio. You may stay at any stage with any routine for as long as you want.

No stage authorizes Claude to send, post, book or cancel an appointment, accept a client, promise a design or price, collect or refund money, make a health or safety judgment, delete, or change an outside system. Use Manual mode for every new or changed routine until you review it again and approve its exact sources, output, and limits.

Only the owner may advance, pause, move back, resume, or retire a routine.

## Step 0. Readiness

Name the owner, choose one repeated admin job, and identify the current inquiry, approved service details, consult notes, waitlist preference, or image permission Claude may read. Define the local draft you want and what should stop the work. Start with one inquiry reply or consult brief. Stop if consent, age eligibility, artist, date, price, design rights, health information, or responsible owner are unclear.

## 1. Assisted

Work beside Claude on one inquiry reply using `skills/draft-an-inquiry-reply/`, or one consult brief using `skills/prepare-a-consult-brief/`.

- Provide the real inquiry and confirmed studio terms.
- Check every artist, date, service, amount, permission, and promise.
- Review the draft and receipt before you contact, accept, or book anyone yourself.

Move only this routine forward when you have seen enough accurate work to trust its pattern.

## 2. Repeatable

Run a small manual batch of inquiry replies, consult briefs, waitlist drafts, cancellation-fill drafts, or recent-work post drafts.

- Keep each client and appointment separate.
- Let missing consent, age, artist, design, or appointment information block only that item.
- Review the final drafts and owner decisions together.

## 3. Supervised operations

Turn one proven reports-only routine into a schedule. Start with the morning brief, end-of-day close, or cancellation-fill review.

Confirm the sources, run time, expected report, and pause conditions first. Every client acceptance, booking, cancellation, message, post, payment, and refund remains your action.

## 4. Intent-led Business OS

Ask Claude to show which inquiries, consults, openings, permissions, or drafts need your attention. Review one exception brief with the evidence and next decision.

Claude may surface unanswered inquiries, missing consult details, open slots, permission gaps, or drafts waiting for review. It never gives health guidance, decides eligibility, accepts a client, books, posts, or contacts anyone.

## Safe next uses

- Prepare waitlist outreach from owner-approved preferences.
- Draft cancellation-fill notes for owner-selected people.
- Draft recent-work posts only for images approved for sharing.

## Stop or defer

- Pause if consent, age, artist, design, image permission, appointment facts, or prices are stale or disputed.
- Defer health and safety judgment, eligibility, client acceptance, bookings, cancellations, payments, refunds, publication, access changes, and deletion.
- Return any routine to an earlier stage whenever you want more hands-on review.
