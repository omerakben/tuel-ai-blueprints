# Draft a recent work post

A caption for a healed piece you picked, with the client's yes already in hand.
Claude writes it in your voice; you post it.

## When to run this

- A healed photo came back and the client said they're happy to share it.
- The feed has been quiet and there's a piece with a confirmed yes waiting its turn.
- You just saw a healed result you're proud of and want the caption while the
  feeling's fresh.

## What Claude reads first

- The photo in assets/ — you name which one.
- The client's yes — a message you paste, or a note already saved in
  business/clients.md. This gets checked before a word is drafted.
- business/staff.md — who tattooed the piece, for the credit line.
- business/brand.md — your voice and your usual tags.
- business/profile.md — how people get in touch, for the closing line.
- templates/work-post.md — the caption shape.

## Steps

1. You pick the piece: which photo in assets/, who tattooed it, and the client's
   first name.
2. Claude confirms the yes. You point to it — a pasted message or a clients.md note.
   No confirmed yes means no draft; the caption can wait until the yes is in.
3. Claude asks where the design came from if that isn't clear: custom, the client's
   own idea, flash, or based on something else. The caption credits it honestly —
   someone else's design is never presented as the studio's own or as original.
4. Claude drafts the caption from templates/work-post.md: what the piece is, one
   true detail worth telling, the artist's credit, how to get in touch, and your
   usual tags. The client's story appears only if their yes covered it.
5. Claude shows you the caption next to the photo's file name. You adjust or approve.
6. The approved caption is saved to reports/ with a note of where the yes came
   from — handy if anyone ever asks.
7. You post it. Claude never posts anywhere.

## What you get

One file saved as reports/recent-work-post-YYYY-MM-DD.md: the approved caption ready
to paste, the photo's file name, the credit, and a line recording the client's yes.

## Never

- Never draft a post about client work without that client's confirmed yes on
  record. "They won't mind" is not a yes, and an old yes for one photo does not
  cover the next one.
- Never present someone else's protected design as the studio's own or as original.
  Flash, references, and another artist's work get credited plainly, or the post
  doesn't go up.
- Never include the client's full name or anything their yes didn't cover.
- Never put healing progress, aftercare tips, or any medical talk in a caption.
- Never post, and never schedule a post. Posting is always your move.

Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
