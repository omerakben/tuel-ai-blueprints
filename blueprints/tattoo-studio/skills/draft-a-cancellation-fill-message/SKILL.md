# Draft a cancellation fill message

A slot just opened — a cancellation, a no-show, a session that ran short. Claude
drafts the offers to your waitlist so the chair doesn't sit empty. You send them.

## When to run this

- A cancellation just landed and you want the slot filled today or this week.
- The cancellation-fill schedule found an opening you flagged and handed off here.
- A session wrapped early and the rest of the day has a workable gap.

## What Claude reads first

- The opening itself — the day, the time, how long, and whose chair. This comes from
  you or from the note you left in assets/; Claude never assumes a slot is open.
- Your waitlist — pasted, read out, or a copy in assets/.
- business/clients.md — who asked to be contacted and any "don't message" notes.
- business/brand.md — your voice, so each offer sounds like you.
- templates/cancellation-fill-text.md — the shape each offer starts from.

## Steps

1. Confirm the opening: when, how long, with which artist. If any piece of that is
   missing, Claude asks before going further.
2. Shortlist who fits. A big piece doesn't fit a two-hour gap; Claude matches the
   time to the piece using only what the waitlist says. Only people who asked to
   hear from you make the shortlist.
3. Show you the shortlist with the reason each person is on it. You trim it or
   reorder it — who hears first is your call.
4. Draft one message per approved person from templates/cancellation-fill-text.md —
   short, personal, naming their piece. If the offer goes to more than one person,
   every message says the slot goes to the first to reply.
5. Show you the batch. You approve by name, then send each message yourself. Claude
   never sends, and never queues anything to go out later.
6. Save the run to reports/ with a "sent?" line under each draft so you can tick off
   what went out.
7. When someone takes the slot, tell Claude — it marks the report and offers a
   one-line clients.md update for your yes.

## What you get

One file saved as reports/cancellation-fill-YYYY-MM-DD.md: the opening, the shortlist
and why each person is on it, every approved message ready to copy, and a "sent?"
line under each.

## Never

- Never message anyone the moment a slot opens. The shortlist and every draft wait
  for your fresh yes — speed never skips approval.
- Never offer one slot to several people without saying "first to reply gets it".
- Never skip the studio's usual steps to fill a chair fast — the consult, the
  deposit, and the age and ID check happen exactly as they always do.
- Never state a price or a deposit amount unless you gave it for this exact message.
- Never invent an opening or a waitlist entry, and never send a single message —
  that part is yours.

Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
