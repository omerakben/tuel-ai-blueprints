# Prepare waitlist outreach

From the waitlist you keep, Claude builds a who-to-contact list with a short drafted
note for each person. You look it over, cross names off, and send the ones you like.

## When to run this

- The weeks ahead have room and the waitlist has been waiting.
- The weekly bookings hour turned up gaps worth filling.
- A project wrapped early and there's suddenly space in the book.

For a single slot that just opened today, use skills/draft-a-cancellation-fill-message/
instead — it moves faster.

## What Claude reads first

- Your waitlist. Claude cannot see it on its own — paste it, read it out, or drop a
  copy in assets/. It stays wherever you keep it; Claude only works from the copy you
  share.
- business/clients.md — who asked to be contacted and any "don't message" notes.
- business/policies.md — your limits on reaching out.
- business/brand.md — your voice, so each note sounds like you.
- templates/cancellation-fill-text.md — the starting shape for each note.

## Steps

1. Ask for the waitlist and the openings. Claude works only from what you give it —
   no list, no outreach.
2. Confirm what's on offer. Real openings from your book get named days and times. If
   nothing is confirmed yet, Claude asks whether to draft "room is opening up" notes
   instead, or wait.
3. Build the who-to-contact list: each person, their piece, and the opening that fits
   them. Only people who asked to hear from you make the list; anyone who ever said
   stop stays off, however long they've waited.
4. Show you the list before a word is drafted. Cross off anyone you like; Claude waits
   for your go-ahead.
5. Draft one note per approved person — short, personal, naming their piece — with an
   easy way out in every note, like "if you'd rather not hear from us, say so and
   I'll stop".
6. Show you the whole batch. You edit or cut any note, approve the keepers by name,
   and send them yourself from your own phone or account.
7. Save the run to reports/ so you can tick people off as replies come in.
8. If the list turned up something durable — someone moved away, someone's piece
   changed — Claude proposes the clients.md update and waits for your yes.

## What you get

One file saved as reports/waitlist-outreach-YYYY-MM-DD.md: the who-to-contact list
with the reason each person is on it, one approved note per person ready to copy and
send, and a skipped list with the reason for each skip.

## Never

- Never invent a waitlist entry, an opening, or how long someone has waited. The list
  you keep is the only list.
- Never draft for anyone who didn't ask to be contacted, or who ever said stop.
- Never reshuffle who hears first without saying so — if two people fit one opening,
  that call is yours.
- Never send a note or set one up to send later. Drafts live in the report; your
  hands do the sending.
- Never put a price or a date promise in a note unless you gave it for that exact note.

Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
