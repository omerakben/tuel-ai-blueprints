# Cancellation fill text

A short personal message offering an opened slot to someone on the waitlist. Claude drafts it; the owner sends it.

## The skeleton

Hi [first name]! A spot just opened up at [studio name] — [day] at [rough time], enough for [their piece, or the next session on it]. You're on the list, so you get first crack. Want it? [If it's going to more than one person: "First to reply gets it, so no hard feelings either way."] [An easy out, like "If you'd rather not get texts from us, say so and I'll stop."]

## How to fill it in

- Two or three sentences. It's a text, not a letter.
- It should read like the owner typed it between sessions. If it sounds like a company wrote it, redo it.
- Only people from the owner's waitlist who asked to be contacted. Not sure someone did? Ask the owner first.
- When several people get the offer, every message says the slot goes to the first reply.
- No prices and no deposit amounts unless the owner gave them for this exact message.
- Show the owner every message before anything is sent. They send each one themselves.
