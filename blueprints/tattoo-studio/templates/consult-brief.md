# Consult brief

One page the artist reads before a consult. Facts and the client's own words only — the design decisions happen at the consult, not on this page.

## The skeleton

**Client:** [first name]

**Consult:** [day and time, from the owner's book]

**How they got in touch:** [where the inquiry came from]

**The idea, in their words:** ["quote them exactly — do not polish it"]

**Placement:** [what they said]

**Size:** [what they said — everyday terms are fine]

**References:** [file names in assets/, or "none in the folder yet"]

**Their questions:** [anything they asked that's waiting on the artist]

**Still missing:** [what the artist may want to ask at the consult]

## How to fill it in

- Quote the client. Their words carry what they actually want; a paraphrase starts making design calls.
- No style suggestions and no placement opinions — a blank is a question for the consult, never a recommendation.
- Health stays out. If the client raised anything health-related, write one line — "raised a health question, one for you at the consult" — and no details.
- If a reference looks like another artist's finished tattoo, say so plainly on the reference line.
- No pricing and no date promises anywhere on the page.
