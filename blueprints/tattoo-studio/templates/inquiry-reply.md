# Inquiry reply

A warm reply to someone asking about getting tattooed. Claude drafts it and shows the owner. Only the owner sends it.

## The skeleton

Hi [first name] — thanks for reaching out! [One warm line about their idea, in their words.]

To point [artist name] in the right direction, could you tell me a little more?

- Roughly how big are you thinking? [An everyday comparison helps — palm-sized, a full forearm.]
- Where on the body?
- Got any reference pictures? Send over anything that shows the feel you're after.

[Closing line about what happens next — for example: "Once these are in, [artist name] will take a look and come back to you on pricing and timing. Every piece gets priced after we've seen the idea."]

## How to fill it in

- Sound like brand.md. If the reply could have come from any studio in town, redo it.
- Never a price and never a date — those are the artist's call, made after seeing the idea. If the owner wants one in this exact reply, they say it and it goes in exactly as said.
- If the message raises age at all, state the studio's age and ID rule from policies.md plainly and kindly. No workarounds, ever.
- Nothing medical — no comments on skin, healing, or allergies. An aftercare question gets the studio's own printed sheet quoted word for word, or a note that the artist will cover it in person.
- Show the owner the exact reply before it goes anywhere. They send it.
