# Fill a cancelled slot

A slot just opened. This drafts a short personal offer to each waitlisted parent —
one message per parent, ready for you to send.

## When to run this

- Someone just cancelled and the slot is fresh.
- A no-show left a gap you'd rather not eat.
- You spot a hole in tomorrow's book and want it filled honestly.

## What Claude reads first

- The open slot: day, time, and how long it is. You say it or paste the book.
- business/clients.md — the waitlist, who's due back soon, and who said texts are okay.
- business/services.md — which grooms fit the slot by length.
- business/policies.md — your rules on holds and deposits, and your hard limits.
- business/brand.md — your voice, so each message sounds like you.
- templates/reminder-text.md — the starting shape for each message.

## Steps

1. Confirm the slot. Claude repeats back the day, time, and length. If any of that
   is fuzzy, it asks before drafting a word.
2. Pick who to offer it to. Claude proposes a short list from clients.md: waitlisted
   parents first, then regulars whose pup is due back and whose groom fits the slot.
   You cross off or add anyone.
3. Keep only parents who said texts are okay. Anyone who asked for space stays off
   the list, whatever the gap costs.
4. Draft one message per approved parent. Each one names the pet, offers the slot as
   "first to reply gets it", and carries an easy out like "reply STOP and I won't
   text again". No two read the same.
5. Show you the batch. You edit or cut any draft, then approve the keepers by name.
   You send them from your own phone, one at a time, in the order you choose.
6. Save the report to reports/ so you can mark who answered.
7. When someone takes the slot, tell Claude — it drafts a quick "slot's taken,
   you're top of the list next time" note for the others, again for you to send.

## What you get

One file saved as reports/cancellation-fill-YYYY-MM-DD.md:

- The open slot and how it came free.
- The offer list, in the order you approved.
- Every approved draft, word for word, with a "sent?" line under each.
- Who took the slot, once you tell Claude.

## Never

- Never send a message or set one up to go out later — every offer leaves from
  your phone.
- Never hide that a slot is going to several parents; every draft offers it as
  "first to reply".
- Never text a parent who didn't say texts are okay, or who ever said stop.
- Never invent a discount to sweeten the offer. If it isn't in policies.md or
  something you just said, Claude asks.
- Never offer the slot to a pet whose groom doesn't fit the time — a rushed groom
  is nobody's win, and the fit comes from services.md, not hope.
- Never use a message like this to raise a health or behavior worry — no diagnosis,
  no treatment talk. The vet gets those, kindly, and never by text.

Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
