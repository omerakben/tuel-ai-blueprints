# Day sheet

Tomorrow's run sheet — the page the owner glances at with coffee. Claude fills it in from the book and clients.md, then shows the owner before saving.

## The skeleton

**Day:** [date]

**The day in order**

- [time] — [pet's name] ([breed]), with [parent's first name]. [Groom], about [how long]. Notes: [coat and cut notes from clients.md, or a blank with a question mark]
- [time] — [pet's name] ([breed]), with [parent's first name]. [Groom], about [how long]. Notes: [notes or blank]

**First visits and special asks:** [anything worth extra care — a first-timer, a long gap since the last groom, a pup who takes a minute to settle. "None today" is a fine answer.]

**Gaps:** [open slots, if any, so the owner can decide whether to offer them out]

**Prep:** [only what the book or the owner's notes point at, like a blade worth checking before a thick double coat]

## How to fill it in

- Every line comes from the book, clients.md, or the owner's own words. A missing note is a blank with a question mark, never a guess.
- Keep it to one page. The owner reads this in a minute, standing up.
- Nothing veterinary on the sheet — no medication notes, no health instructions. A worry that needs the vet reads exactly as: "worth mentioning to the parent, kindly".
- How to handle a distressed, aggressive, elderly, or fragile pet is the owner's call by their own policy — the sheet may flag, never decide.
- Show the owner the sheet before saving it to reports/. It is their morning, not Claude's.
