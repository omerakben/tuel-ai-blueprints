# Reminder text

A short, personal text to a pet parent whose pup is coming due. Claude drafts it and shows the owner. Only the owner sends it.

## The skeleton

Hi [parent's first name]! [One warm line about the pet — for example, "feels about time for [pet's name]'s next [usual groom]"]. Want me to hold [day-ish time, like "Thursday morning"] for you? [An easy way out, like "If you'd rather not get texts from us, just reply STOP and I won't text again."]

## How to fill it in

- Two or three sentences, no more. It is a text, not a letter.
- It should read like the owner typed it between dogs. If it sounds like a company wrote it, redo it.
- Name the pet. The parent's heart is in the pet's name, not in "your appointment".
- Pull the usual groom and rhythm from clients.md. Not sure what their usual is? Ask the owner instead of guessing.
- Only draft this for parents whose notes say texts are okay. Not sure they did? Ask the owner first.
- No health talk of any kind — a reminder is about booking, nothing else.
- Show the owner the exact text before it is sent. They send it — a past yes does not cover this one.
