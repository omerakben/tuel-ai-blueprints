Claude works through this over time — never as a form, always as conversation. Tier 1 is enough for the first win. Everything else can wait.

## Tier 1 — needed for the first win

- Business name
- How the grooming happens (a shop, a mobile van, house calls, or a mix) and the feel of the place in three words
- Days and grooming hours
- Who grooms — just you? that's fine — plus what each person is great at and which days, if there's a crew
- Main grooms with usual price, rough time, and about how many weeks before that pet is due back
- How pet parents book today (calls, texts, social messages, a booking tool)
- A few regulars: pet's name, parent's first name, usual groom, roughly when they last came in, and whether texts are okay

## Tier 2 — makes it good

- The full groom and price list (photo, file, or pasted — any shape is fine)
- House policies: cancellations, no-shows, late pickup, matted coats and shave-downs, extra fees, and what the owner does for anxious, aggressive, elderly, or fragile pets
- More regulars: coat and cut preferences, how each pet does with grooming (only in the parent's or the owner's own words), the usual rhythm
- The waitlist: parents who want to hear when a slot opens
- Suppliers: who provides shampoo and product, who sharpens blades and shears, how orders get placed
- Logo and a few photos of finished grooms, dropped into `assets/`
- The business's look and voice: three words, colors, phrases the owner loves, phrases to avoid, and the tags they usually put on posts
- Where reviews land (Google, Yelp) and whether the owner wants help replying

## Tier 3 — nice to have

- Reorder points: how low shampoo or product gets before it's time to order
- Retail sold at pickup, if any
- Busiest and slowest days, as the owner sees them
- The business's story — how it started, what it's known for
- How each groomer takes their pay (commission, hourly), only if the owner wants the day's close to track it
- Anything with a renewal date the owner wants nudges about (license, insurance, van servicing)
