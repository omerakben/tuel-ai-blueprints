# Your AI adoption map

This map helps a pet grooming business add Claude work at a pace the owner can trust. Maturity belongs to each routine, not to the whole business. You may stay at any stage with any routine for as long as you want.

No stage authorizes Claude to send, book or cancel an appointment, promise a service or time, charge or refund, make a health or safety judgment, delete, or change an outside system. Use Manual mode for every new or changed routine until you review it again and approve its exact sources, output, and limits.

Only the owner may advance, pause, move back, resume, or retire a routine.

## Step 0. Readiness

Name the owner, choose one repeated admin job, and identify the current grooming request, confirmed appointment list, client preference, or review Claude may read. Define the local draft you want and what should stop the work. Start with one grooming-request reply or rebooking reminder. Stop if consent, service, date, price, contact preference, pet behavior concern, health information, or responsible owner are unclear.

## 1. Assisted

Work beside Claude on one request reply using `skills/draft-a-groom-request-reply/`, or one reminder using `skills/draft-a-rebooking-reminder/`.

- Provide the real request and approved service facts.
- Check every pet and owner detail, service, date, price, and promise.
- Review the draft and receipt before you contact or book anyone yourself.

Move only this routine forward when you have seen enough accurate work to trust its pattern.

## 2. Repeatable

Run a small manual batch of request replies, rebooking reminders, cancellation-fill drafts, day preparation notes, or review replies.

- Keep each pet, owner, and appointment separate.
- Let missing consent, preference, or appointment information block only that item.
- Review the final drafts and owner decisions together.

## 3. Supervised operations

Turn one proven reports-only routine into a schedule. Start with the morning brief, end-of-day close, or rebook push.

Confirm the sources, run time, expected report, and pause conditions first. Every message, booking, cancellation, service decision, charge, and refund remains your action.

## 4. Intent-led Business OS

Ask Claude to show which appointments, rebooking opportunities, or drafts need your attention. Review one exception brief with the evidence and next decision.

Claude may surface missing appointment details, due-back reminders, open slots, or replies waiting for review. It never evaluates a pet's health or behavior, books, cancels, charges, or contacts anyone.

## Safe next uses

- Prepare the groom-day plan from confirmed appointments.
- Draft cancellation-fill notes from an owner-approved waitlist.
- Draft review replies from specific reviews.

## Stop or defer

- Pause if appointment facts, consent, preferences, contact choices, or service details are stale or disputed.
- Defer health, behavior, and safety judgment, bookings, cancellations, service decisions, charges, refunds, customer promises, access changes, and deletion.
- Return any routine to an earlier stage whenever you want more hands-on review.
