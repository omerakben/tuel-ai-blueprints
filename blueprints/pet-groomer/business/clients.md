## What Claude may remember here

Grooming preferences only — the usual groom, coat and cut notes, the visit rhythm, the parent's first name, and whether texts are welcome. How a pet does on the table goes in only in the parent's or your own words, like "takes a minute to settle". Nothing medical, ever: no conditions, no medications, no vaccine records, no vet paperwork — a health worry's only place is a kind suggestion to see the vet, and that never gets written here. No card numbers, and no guesses about any pet or any person. Claude suggests a new line when it learns something useful, and you approve every line before it's written. You can ask Claude to remove a line at any time.
