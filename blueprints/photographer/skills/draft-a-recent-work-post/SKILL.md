# Recent work posts

Draft the caption for work you picked and cleared with the client — you post it
when you're happy with it.

## When to run this

When a gallery goes out and the client loves it, or during the weekly rhythm hour
(workflows/weekly-marketing.md). The owner picks the work; Claude writes the words.

## What Claude reads first

- business/brand.md — your voice, your tags, the phrases you avoid.
- business/profile.md — how people reach you, for the closing line.
- business/clients.md — only to check what you've said about sharing.
- templates/promo-post.md — the shape the caption starts from.

## Steps

1. The owner names the shoot and the photos. Claude never picks a client's photos
   for a post on its own.
2. Confirm consent in this chat, every time: did the client agree to sharing —
   and if a child is in the photos, did a parent or guardian agree? A note in
   clients.md is a good sign; the owner saying so now is the yes that counts.
   No confirmation, no draft.
3. Ask the owner for one true thing about the shoot — what the client was
   celebrating, what the light did, what made the day. The caption is built from
   that, never from invention.
4. Draft the caption from templates/promo-post.md in the voice of brand.md. No
   client names unless the owner says to use them, and no made-up client quotes,
   ever.
5. Show the draft and wait. The owner edits or approves; changed drafts come back
   for another look. The owner posts it — Claude never does.
6. Save the caption to reports/ so it's there when the owner is ready to post.
7. If the owner wants the client's sharing yes kept on file, propose the
   clients.md line and wait for approval before writing it.

## What you get

One file in reports/, named like reports/recent-work-post-YYYY-MM-DD.md. Inside:

- The caption, ready to copy when the owner posts.
- Which photos the owner picked, by the names the owner used.
- A one-line record of the consent the owner confirmed, in the owner's words.

## Never

- Never pick a client's photos for a post. The owner picks; Claude writes.
- Never draft around missing consent. No owner confirmation that the client
  agreed to sharing — and a parent or guardian for any child — means no draft,
  full stop.
- Never name or tag a client unless the owner says to.
- Never invent a client quote, a reaction, or a story that didn't happen.
- Never post, never schedule a post, and never send the caption anywhere.
  Posting is the owner's move.

Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
