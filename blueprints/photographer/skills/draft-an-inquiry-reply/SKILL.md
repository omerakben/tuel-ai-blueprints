# Inquiry replies

Paste in an inquiry and get a warm reply drafted — one that answers from your real
price list and gathers the date, the location, and what they're dreaming of. You send it.

## When to run this

The moment an inquiry lands — an email, a message, a note from your website form.
Paste it in as is. It also runs each morning inside schedules/inquiry-sweep.md for
anything that arrived overnight.

## What Claude reads first

- business/services.md — your sessions, prices, and what's included, so the reply
  answers with real numbers, never guesses.
- business/profile.md — where you shoot and the area you cover.
- business/policies.md — deposits and booking terms, in case the inquiry asks.
- business/brand.md — how you talk, so the draft sounds like you.
- templates/inquiry-reply.md — the shape each reply starts from.

## Steps

1. Take the inquiry as pasted. Pull out what the person asked: the kind of shoot,
   any date or place they mentioned, and the question they actually want answered.
2. Check services.md for every fact the reply needs. If a package fact is missing —
   a price, what's included, how long a session runs — stop and ask the owner
   before drafting. No fact, no draft.
3. Draft the reply from templates/inquiry-reply.md: answer their question straight
   from services.md, then gather the three things — the date they have in mind,
   where they picture the shoot, and what they're dreaming of.
4. Leave availability open. Claude never says a date is free or taken; the calendar
   is the owner's. If the owner already said the date works, the reply may say so
   in the owner's words.
5. Show the draft and wait. The owner edits or approves; changed drafts come back
   for another look. The owner sends it from their own email or messages.
6. Save the report to reports/ so the thread has a home when the reply comes back.
7. If the inquiry holds something durable — a family with a fall rhythm, a client
   who found you through a friend — propose the line for business/clients.md and
   wait for a yes before writing it.

## What you get

One file in reports/, a fresh one each run, named like
reports/inquiry-reply-YYYY-MM-DD.md. Inside:

- The inquiry as it arrived, so nothing gets lost.
- The drafted reply, ready to copy and send.
- Anything Claude still needs from you, one question per line.

## Never

- Never invent a price, a package, or an open date. If services.md is silent, ask.
- Never say a date is free or taken. Availability is the owner's call, every time.
- Never quote a deposit, cancellation, or photo-usage term that isn't in
  policies.md or the owner's own words.
- Never promise photos, timing, or extras the owner hasn't offered.
- Never send the reply, and never set one up to send later. The owner presses send.

Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
