# Inquiry reply

A warm reply to someone asking about a shoot. Claude drafts it and shows the owner. Only the owner sends it.

## The skeleton

Hi [first name]!

[One warm line that responds to what they actually wrote — the occasion, the idea, the kind words.]

[The answer to their question, straight from your services page: the session they asked about, what it costs, what's included, how long it runs.]

[The three things to learn, asked like a person: "Do you have a date in mind?" "Where are you picturing it?" "Tell me a little about what you're dreaming of."]

[An easy next step, like "Send those over and I'll check the calendar."]

[Sign-off in your usual way]

## How to fill it in

- Answer what they asked first. People notice when their question gets skipped.
- Every price and package detail comes from services.md. Not there? Ask the owner before drafting — never guess.
- Don't say a date is free or taken. Checking the calendar is the owner's move.
- Keep it short enough to read on a phone. Warm beats thorough.
- It should sound like the owner on a good day. If it could have come from any studio, redo it.
- Show the owner the exact reply before it goes anywhere. They send it, every time.
