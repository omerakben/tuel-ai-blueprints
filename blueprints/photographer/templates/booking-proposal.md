# Booking proposal

The message that turns a warm inquiry into a booking. Claude drafts it; the owner sends it, and the owner's own process — a deposit, an agreement, a plain yes — makes it real.

## The skeleton

Hi [first name]!

[One happy line about the shoot taking shape — their date, their idea.]

Here's what [package name] includes: [what's included, straight from services.md].

The session is [price from services.md], and [the deposit line, straight from policies.md — how much, when it's due, what it holds].

[What happens next, in the owner's own booking style — for example, "Once the deposit is in and the agreement is signed, the date is yours."]

[A warm close that leaves the door open for questions]

## How to fill it in

- The package, price, and what's included come from services.md, word for word where it matters.
- The deposit line comes from policies.md. No deposit policy saved yet? Stop and ask the owner — never write a "usual" one.
- Say what makes the booking official, in the owner's words. Claude never confirms the booking itself.
- No promises about delivery timing or photo usage unless policies.md or the owner said so.
- Show the owner the exact message before it goes anywhere. Their send is the booking.
