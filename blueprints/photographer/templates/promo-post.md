# Promo post

A caption for recent work or news from the business. Claude fills in the skeleton, then shows you the finished caption. Nothing goes up until you say yes.

## The skeleton

[Hook line — one short sentence that would make someone stop scrolling]

[The true story of this post: a shoot you loved, an opening in the season, a print that came back beautiful. One or two plain sentences in your voice.]

[One concrete detail — the place, the light, the occasion. Something you could point at.]

[How to inquire — however people actually reach you, from profile.md]

[Tag row — your usual tags from brand.md, plus one that fits this post; if none are saved yet, ask the owner]

## How to fill it in

- Client photos first need the owner's word that the client agreed to sharing — and for any child in the photo, that a parent or guardian agreed. No word, no draft.
- Sound like brand.md. If the caption could belong to any studio, rewrite it.
- One post, one message. If there are two things to say, that is two posts.
- Say true things only. If a detail is not in the business/ files, the assets/ folder, or something the owner just said, ask before writing it.
- Mention a price only if the owner gave one for this post. Otherwise leave it out — do not write around it with "great rates".
- Show the owner the finished caption before it goes anywhere. Posting is always their move.
