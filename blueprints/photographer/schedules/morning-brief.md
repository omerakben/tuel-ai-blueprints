# Morning brief

A short rundown of your day before it starts, saved where you can find it again.

Mode: runs on your computer — Claude Desktop and this folder need to be open.

Drafts only — nothing goes out on a timer. <!-- action level: B -->

## How to turn it on

In Claude Desktop, create a scheduled task and pick a time before your day starts — half an hour before you'd naturally check your messages works well. Paste the prompt below as the task's instructions, exactly as written.

You don't need the schedule, though. Any morning, open this folder and say the prompt yourself. Same brief either way.

One thing the brief needs: what's on today. Paste today's plan into the chat, or keep your booking notes in the assets/ folder. If Claude can't tell what today holds, it asks you instead of guessing.

## The prompt

```text
Time for the morning brief. Read the business/ folder first — it is the source of truth for this photography business.

Then:

1. Read yesterday's end-of-day report in reports/, if there is one.
2. Find today's plan. Check assets/ for it, or use what I have pasted here. If you can't tell what today holds, stop and ask me. Do not guess.
3. Write a brief of 6 to 8 lines covering: any shoot today and what its brief in reports/ says to remember, inquiries still waiting on a reply, drafts in reports/ still waiting on my yes, galleries or edits I said I owe someone, and one small suggestion for the day.
4. Save it as reports/morning-brief-[today's date].md.

Use only what is in business/, assets/, yesterday's report, or what I tell you. If a fact is not there, ask me. Never invent names or prices.

End by listing anything that needs my decision, one per line. If nothing does, say so.
```
