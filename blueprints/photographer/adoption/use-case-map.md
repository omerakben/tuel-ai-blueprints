# Your AI adoption map

This map helps a photography business add Claude work at a pace the owner can trust. Maturity belongs to each routine, not to the whole business. You may stay at any stage with any routine for as long as you want.

No stage authorizes Claude to send, book a shoot, promise a date or deliverable, publish an image, collect a deposit, pay, sign, delete, or change an outside system. Use Manual mode for every new or changed routine until you review it again and approve its exact sources, output, and limits.

Only the owner may advance, pause, move back, resume, or retire a routine.

## Step 0. Readiness

Name the owner, choose one repeated job, and identify the current inquiry, approved package list, shoot notes, image consent, or booking status Claude may read. Define the local draft you want and what should stop the work. Start with one inquiry reply or shoot brief. Stop if scope, date, package, price, usage permission, delivery term, or responsible owner are unclear.

## 1. Assisted

Work beside Claude on one inquiry reply using `skills/draft-an-inquiry-reply/`, or one shoot brief using `skills/prepare-a-shoot-brief/`.

- Provide the real inquiry and confirmed business terms.
- Check every date, package, amount, deliverable, usage term, and promise.
- Review the draft and receipt before you contact or book anyone yourself.

Move only this routine forward when you have seen enough accurate work to trust its pattern.

## 2. Repeatable

Run a small manual batch of inquiry replies, booking proposals, shoot briefs, rebooking notes, or recent-work post drafts.

- Keep each client and shoot separate.
- Let a missing date, package, consent, or usage term block only that item.
- Review the final drafts and owner decisions together.

## 3. Supervised operations

Turn one proven reports-only routine into a schedule. Start with the morning brief, end-of-day close, or inquiry sweep.

Confirm the sources, run time, expected report, and pause conditions first. Every booking, message, publication, deposit, payment, signature, and client promise remains your action.

## 4. Intent-led Business OS

Ask Claude to show which inquiries, shoots, deliverables, or drafts need your attention. Review one exception brief with the evidence and next decision.

Claude may surface unanswered inquiries, missing shoot details, approaching dates, consent gaps, or drafts waiting for review. It never books, publishes images, commits to delivery, collects money, or contacts anyone.

## Safe next uses

- Draft booking proposals from approved packages.
- Draft rebooking notes from owner-confirmed client history.
- Summarize weekly marketing and flag missing permissions.

## Stop or defer

- Pause if dates, packages, consent, usage rights, deliverables, or client instructions are stale or disputed.
- Defer contract and rights judgment, bookings, publication, deposits, payments, signatures, customer promises, access changes, and deletion.
- Return any routine to an earlier stage whenever you want more hands-on review.
