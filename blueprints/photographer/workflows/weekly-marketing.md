# Weekly rhythm hour

One hour a week to plan your posts and rebooking notes — Claude drafts them, and you decide what goes out.

## When

Once a week, at a time you pick. Many photographers use the morning after a delivery day, when the work is fresh and next month's calendar is on their mind. Say "let's do the rhythm hour" and Claude starts.

## Steps

1. Look back. Claude reads last week's plan in reports/ and checks what went out and what people responded to. If there is no record yet, Claude says so and starts fresh.
2. Find something real to talk about. Claude looks for actual happenings: a gallery that just went out, a season filling up, openings in the calendar, a print or album back from the lab. Real events only. If nothing stands out, Claude asks what is going on this week instead of inventing something.
3. Clear the photos. For any post built on client work, you confirm the client agreed to sharing — and for a child in the photos, that a parent or guardian did. Work without that confirmation stays out of the plan, however good it looks.
4. Pick the angle together. Claude suggests one or two angles and says why. You choose, or name your own. Nothing moves forward until you have picked.
5. Draft the captions. Claude writes 2 or 3 using templates/promo-post.md, in your voice from business/brand.md. Sessions and prices come from business/services.md, never from memory or guesswork.
6. Draft any rebooking notes. If past clients in business/clients.md are due for the next round, Claude drafts their notes using skills/draft-a-rebooking-note/. Names and rhythms come from clients.md only.
7. Approve the batch. Claude lays everything out in one place — every caption and every note, together. You go item by item: say yes or no, or tell Claude what to change. Changed items come back for another look. An item you skip is a no. Only items with your fresh yes make the plan.
8. Make the posting plan. Claude ends with a short plan: each approved item, the day and rough time you will post or send it, and where it goes. You do the posting. The plan and the approved drafts are saved to reports/.

## What you get

- 2 or 3 captions and any rebooking notes, approved by you and ready to copy.
- A one-page posting plan saved as reports/weekly-marketing-[date].md, with the approved drafts underneath it.

## Never

- Claude never posts or sends anything. Not one word leaves this folder without you.
- No client photo enters the plan without your confirmation that the client agreed to sharing — and a parent or guardian for any child. A note on file is not the confirmation; you saying so is.
- Claude never invents an event, a discount, a price, or a happy client. If it is not in your business/ files, your assets/ folder, or something you just said, Claude asks.
- A yes covers that exact item, that week. Last week's yes approves nothing this week, and staying quiet on an item is a no.
- Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
