# Draft the bake post

Turns today's bake into a ready-to-post caption — you paste it and post it when you're happy.

## When to run this

Something worth showing just came out of the oven: the sourdough turned out beautiful, the fruit tarts are stacked in the case, a special is running today. Any moment you'd normally think "I should post this" — say so instead, and Claude drafts it.

## What Claude reads first

- business/brand.md — your voice, your phrases, your usual tags, and your exact allergen words if you've saved any.
- business/services.md — the bake's real name and price, if you want the price in the post.
- business/profile.md — your hours and how people find you.
- templates/bake-post.md — the shape the caption starts from.
- assets/ — if you dropped a photo in, Claude can point the caption at it.

## Steps

1. Tell Claude what's in the case: which bake, how many there are, and how long they'll last — "a dozen cinnamon rolls until they're gone" is plenty.
2. If the item or its availability is missing, Claude stops and asks. No bake named, no post. No sense of how many or until when, no post either. Claude never pads a caption with urgency it can't back up.
3. Claude drafts one caption using templates/bake-post.md, in the voice from business/brand.md, with one concrete true detail — the bake, the batch, the day.
4. A price appears only if you gave one for this post, or it's in services.md and you want it in. Claude asks rather than assumes.
5. Claude shows you the caption and stops. Edit it, swap the detail, or approve it as is.
6. You copy the caption into Instagram or Facebook and post it yourself. Claude never posts.
7. The caption is saved to reports/ so you can find the good ones again.

## What you get

One file in reports/, named reports/bake-post-YYYY-MM-DD.md: the approved caption, the photo it points at if there is one, and a line noting where you planned to post it.

## Never

- Never post, schedule a post, or queue one up anywhere. You post, every time.
- Never invent a bake, a batch size, a price, or availability. If it isn't in business/, in assets/, or in what you just said, Claude asks.
- Never make allergen or diet claims in a caption — nothing is called nut-free, gluten-free, or safe for anyone — unless you wrote those exact words in business/ and want them in, repeated as your words.
- Never claim a bake keeps until tomorrow or lasts the week. Shelf life is yours to say, not Claude's to guess.
- Never reuse yesterday's yes. A new post means a fresh look and a fresh okay.

Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
