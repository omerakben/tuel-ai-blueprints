# Your AI adoption map

This map helps a bakery add Claude work at a pace the owner can trust. Maturity belongs to each routine, not to the whole bakery. You may stay at any stage with any routine for as long as you want.

No stage authorizes Claude to send, post, accept an order, buy ingredients, pay, promise availability, delete, or change an outside system. Use Manual mode for every new or changed routine until you review it again and approve its exact sources, output, and limits.

Only the owner may advance, pause, move back, resume, or retire a routine.

## Step 0. Readiness

Name the owner, choose one repeated job, and identify the current order note, product list, stock count, or bake plan Claude may read. Define the local draft you want and what should stop the work. Start with one custom-order reply or bake list. Stop if the order details, allergen boundary, availability, price, or responsible owner are unclear.

## 1. Assisted

Work beside Claude on one real custom-order reply using `skills/draft-a-custom-order-reply/`, or one bake list using `skills/prepare-the-bake-list/`.

- Provide the current request or confirmed orders.
- Check every item, quantity, pickup detail, price, and limitation.
- Review the draft and receipt before you reply or start production.

Move only this routine forward when you have seen enough accurate work to trust its pattern.

## 2. Repeatable

Run a small manual batch of custom-order replies, bake lists, ingredient-order drafts, or bake-post drafts.

- Keep each customer order separate.
- Let a missing quantity, price, or availability block only that item.
- Review the batch and owner decision list before doing anything outside the folder.

## 3. Supervised operations

Turn one proven reports-only routine into a schedule. Start with the morning brief, end-of-day close, or weekend preorder push.

Confirm the source files, run time, expected report, and pause conditions first. Every order acceptance, post, purchase, and customer promise remains your action.

## 4. Intent-led Business OS

Ask Claude to show what could disrupt the next bake or which drafts need your attention. Review one exception brief with the evidence and next decision.

Claude may surface missing order details, low ingredients, unconfirmed pickups, or drafts waiting for review. It never decides food safety, accepts orders, buys stock, or contacts anyone.

## Safe next uses

- Draft the daily or weekend bake post from confirmed products.
- Prepare an ingredient-order list from a current count.
- Summarize weekly production and flag missing inputs.

## Stop or defer

- Pause if orders, stock counts, availability, or policies are stale or disputed.
- Defer allergen and food-safety decisions, customer promises, ordering, payments, refunds, public posting, access changes, and deletion.
- Return any routine to an earlier stage whenever you want more hands-on review.
