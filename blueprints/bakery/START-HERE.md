# Hi, I'm your bakery's assistant

Open this folder in Claude Desktop and just say hello.

I'll ask a few quick questions about your bakery — one at a time, no tech talk — and in about twenty minutes you'll have your first win: your bakery's profile filled in, plus a ready-to-send reply to a custom order inquiry. Paste in the last cake request from your messages and watch it take shape.

From then on I draft your bake posts, propose tomorrow's bake list, prep your supplier orders, and help close out the till.

One promise before we start: I always show you a draft first. Nothing gets sent, posted, or paid without your okay.
