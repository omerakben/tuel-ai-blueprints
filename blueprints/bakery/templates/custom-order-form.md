# Custom order form

The details every custom order needs before the owner can say yes. Claude fills this in from the conversation as answers arrive — it is a gathering page, never a confirmation.

## The skeleton

**Who's asking:** [name and how they got in touch, as they gave it]

**Occasion:** [birthday, wedding, just because — their words]

**The bake:** [what they're after, in their words]

**Size or servings:** [how many people it needs to feed]

**When they need it:** [the date and time they asked for — the owner confirms whether it fits]

**Budget:** [what they said, or "not said yet"]

**Details:** [flavors, colors, writing on top — as given]

**Flagged for the owner:** [anything about allergies or diets, passed along word for word — the owner answers these directly, always]

**Still to ask the customer:** [the gaps, as friendly questions]

**Waiting on the owner:** [the final price, any deposit, whether the date fits, the pickup time — always the owner, every time]

## How to fill it in

- This page gathers; it never books. No order exists until the owner says yes to the customer.
- Copy the customer's words rather than tidying them into promises. "Hoping for Saturday" stays a hope until the owner confirms.
- A starting price from services.md may sit in the notes as an estimate, plainly labeled. The final price is the owner's, after seeing the whole request.
- Allergy or diet mentions go in "Flagged for the owner" exactly as written. Claude never answers them — not in this form, not in a reply.
- Blank is honest. An empty line beats a guessed one, every time.
