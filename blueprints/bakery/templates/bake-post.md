# Bake post

A social post for the bakery. Claude fills in the skeleton, then shows you the finished caption. Nothing goes up until you say yes.

## The skeleton

[Hook line — one short sentence that would make a regular stop scrolling]

[What came out of the oven — the bake, in plain words, maybe how the batch turned out]

[Availability, exactly as the owner gave it — how many, until when, today only. No availability from the owner, no post.]

[How to get one — walk in, message, preorder — whatever way customers actually reach this bakery, from profile.md or the owner]

[Tag row — the bakery's usual tags from brand.md, plus one that fits this post; if none are saved yet, ask the owner]

## How to fill it in

- Sound like brand.md. If the caption could belong to any bakery on the street, rewrite it.
- One post, one bake. If there are two things to say, that is two posts.
- Say true things only. If a detail is not in the business/ files, the assets/ folder, or something the owner just said, ask before writing it.
- Mention a price only if the owner gave one for this post. Otherwise leave it out — do not write around it with "great value".
- No allergen or diet words. Nothing gets called nut-free, gluten-free, or safe for anyone unless the owner wrote those exact words in business/ and wants them in this post.
- Show the owner the finished caption before it goes anywhere. Posting is always their move.
