# Adopt AI one routine at a time

This guide helps you introduce Claude into the business without handing over decisions or outside actions. A routine is one repeatable piece of work, such as preparing a weekly summary or drafting follow-up notes.

Your business does not have one adoption stage. Each routine has its own stage. A weekly report may be ready for scheduled drafting while a hiring task remains assisted or is kept entirely outside Claude.

You may keep a routine at any stage for as long as it remains useful and safe. Progress is an option, not a target.

## Readiness gate

Before Stage 1, agree on:

- The exact outcome and named owner.
- The approved sources and how fresh they need to be.
- Information that must stay out of the folder.
- The maximum action level and the point where Claude must stop.
- Where the draft and work receipt will be saved.
- A simple baseline, such as current time spent, delay, rework, or missed items.

Use Manual permission for every new or changed routine. The first run must end with a local draft or observation, a reality check, and a work receipt.

## Stage 1: Assisted

You work through one routine with Claude and inspect the sources, assumptions, and result closely. Claude prepares and checks the work. You correct it and decide what happens next.

A routine is ready to leave this stage only when repeated reviewed runs show useful results, visible uncertainty, and no boundary violations.

## Stage 2: Repeatable

A successful routine becomes a clear recipe with a named owner, approved sources, expected output, review step, and failure path. Several routines may be open at once, but each uses its own work packet.

A work packet keeps one routine's request, sources, draft, review note, decisions, and receipt together. Never mix customer records, assumptions, approvals, or outputs between packets.

## Stage 3: Supervised operations

A proven A Observe or B Draft routine may run on an owner-approved rhythm. Claude saves reports, stops on missing or stale input, records failures, and brings decisions to the owner.

Schedules never make outside changes or update approved business memory. A local-folder schedule also depends on Claude Desktop and the folder being available.

## Stage 4: Intent-led Business OS

You set priorities, limits, measures, and stop conditions for a group of proven routines. Claude may coordinate the internal loop from capture through triage, draft, verification, owner-review packet, receipt, and improvement proposal.

The loop stops before outside action. Claude does not send, post, pay, refund, file, sign, decide, delete, change access, or perform licensed judgment. You remain accountable for decisions and actions.

## Evidence before advancement

Use `templates/ai-value-review.md` and `workflows/advance-ai-adoption.md` to recommend one of four outcomes: stay, advance, return to an earlier stage, or retire the routine.

Claude never advances a routine by itself. The named owner reviews the evidence and records the decision. A failure, stale source, unexplained result, boundary violation, or unmanageable review queue returns the routine to Manual permission and the last proven stage.

## Action boundary

Adoption stages expand only A Observe and B Draft work. They do not grant permission for C Internal write. The separate `workflows/review-business-memory.md` may make one reversible memory update only after the named owner freshly approves that exact change. It is not scheduled and does not advance a routine.

D Consequential, E Licensed judgment, and F Destructive always remain human work.
