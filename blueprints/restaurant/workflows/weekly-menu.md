# Weekly menu hour

One hour a week to pick the specials, tidy the menu, and draft the posts that go with them — Claude drafts, you decide.

## When

Once a week, at a time you pick. Many owners use the quiet stretch after the weekend, before the ordering gets done. Say "let's do the menu hour" and Claude starts.

## Steps

1. Look back. Claude reads last week's menu plan in reports/ and asks what ran well, what sold out, and what came back to the kitchen. If there is no record yet, Claude says so and starts fresh.
2. Take stock of the kitchen. Claude asks what's coming in from suppliers this week, what needs using up, and what you feel like cooking. Real ingredients and real plans only — if nothing stands out, Claude asks rather than inventing a dish.
3. Pick the week's specials together. Claude suggests one or two directions from what you said and explains why. You choose, or name your own. Nothing moves forward until you have picked — and every special needs its dish, its price, and how long it runs, from you.
4. Draft the posts. Claude writes one caption per special using templates/menu-special-post.md, in your voice from business/brand.md. Dish words are your words; prices come from you or business/services.md, never from memory.
5. Tidy the menu page. If a price changed or a dish is coming off, Claude shows you the exact lines it would change in business/services.md and waits for your yes before writing.
6. Approve the batch. Claude lays out every caption in one place. You go item by item: yes, no, or change it. Changed items come back for another look. An item you skip is a no. Only items with your fresh yes make the plan.
7. Make the week's plan. Claude ends with a short plan: each approved special, the day it runs, and the day you'll post about it. You do the posting. The plan and the approved captions are saved to reports/.

## What you get

- One caption per special, approved by you and ready to copy.
- A one-page week plan saved as reports/weekly-menu-[date].md, with the approved captions underneath it.

## Never

- Claude never posts or sends anything. Not one word leaves this folder without you.
- Claude never invents a dish, a price, a deal, or how long a special runs. A missing one of those means a question, never a guess.
- No allergen or diet claims in any caption — your words about the dish, word for word, and nothing more. No posts built around drinking more, either.
- A yes covers that exact item, that week. Last week's yes approves nothing this week, and staying quiet on an item is a no.
- Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
