# What to collect, in order

Claude works through this over time — never as a form, always as conversation. Tier 1 is enough for the first win. Everything else can wait.

## Tier 1 — needed for the first win

- Restaurant name
- The kind of food, and the one dish the place is known for
- The feel of the place in three words
- Days and hours, and whether that's lunch, dinner, or both
- Number of tables, or a rough seat count
- Menu mainstays with prices
- Who works here — just the owner, or owner and family, is a fine answer
- How guests get a table today, and where those requests land

## Tier 2 — makes it good

- The full menu with prices (photo, file, or pasted — any shape is fine)
- House policies: large parties, holding tables, no-shows and cancellations
- The regulars: names the owner wants remembered, their usual table, their usual order
- Suppliers: who delivers produce, meat and fish, dry goods, drinks — and how orders get placed
- Logo and a few photos the owner likes, dropped into `assets/`
- The restaurant's voice: three words, phrases the owner loves, phrases to avoid, and the tags they usually put on posts
- Where reviews land (Google, Yelp) and whether the owner wants help replying

## Tier 3 — nice to have

- Reorder points: how low the cooler or pantry gets before it's time to order
- The specials rhythm: how often the special changes, and who decides
- Busiest and quietest nights, as the owner sees them
- The restaurant's story — how it started, what it's known for
- Anything with a renewal date the owner wants nudges about (licenses, inspections)
