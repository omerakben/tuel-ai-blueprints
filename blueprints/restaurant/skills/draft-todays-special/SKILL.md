# Draft today's special

Turn tonight's special into a ready-to-post caption. Three things in, one post out — and if the dish, the price, or how long it runs is missing, Claude stops and asks.

## When to run this

- The special is decided and you want the post written before service swallows the afternoon.
- A dish needs to move: the catch came in big, the soup pot is deep.
- The weekly menu hour picked the specials and you want today's written up fresh.

## What Claude reads first

- business/brand.md — your voice and your usual tags.
- business/services.md — dish names and standing prices, so the caption matches the menu.
- business/profile.md — hours and how guests reach you, for the "come get it" line.
- templates/menu-special-post.md — the shape the caption starts from.
- assets/ — if you point at a photo, it goes in the plan.

## Steps

1. Tell Claude the special: the dish, the price, and how long it runs — tonight only, all weekend, while it lasts. What's in it goes in your words.
2. If any of those three is missing, Claude stops and asks. No caption gets drafted around a hole.
3. Claude drafts one caption from templates/menu-special-post.md, in the voice from business/brand.md. The dish is described in your words only; Claude adds no claims of its own about what is or is not in it.
4. If you named a photo in assets/, the draft says which one goes with the post.
5. Claude shows you the caption and stops. Ask for a shorter one, a warmer one, a different opening line — nothing moves until you say it's good.
6. You copy the caption and post it yourself, wherever you post. Claude never posts.
7. Claude saves the caption to reports/ so a good one can be found again the next time the dish comes around.

## What you get

One ready-to-post caption, saved as reports/todays-special-YYYY-MM-DD.md, with the dish, the price, how long it runs, and the photo pick if there was one.

## Never

- Never drafts around a missing dish, price, or run. Claude asks first, every time.
- Never calls a dish gluten-free, nut-free, vegan, or safe for any allergy on its own. The only diet words in a caption are yours, word for word — and a guest with an allergen question gets pointed to you directly.
- Never builds a post around drinking more. A glass can sit beside the plate in a photo; rounds, drink deals, and anything that rewards another glass stay out.
- Never invents a price or a deal, and never writes "great prices" to dodge a missing one.
- Never posts anything anywhere. You post; Claude drafts.

Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
