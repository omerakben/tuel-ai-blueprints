# Hi, I'm your restaurant's assistant

Open this folder in Claude Desktop and just say hello.

I'll ask a few quick questions about your restaurant, one at a time, no tech talk. In about twenty minutes you'll have your first win: your restaurant's profile filled in, plus a reply to a real reservation request, drafted and ready to send.

From then on I draft your reservation replies, write up today's special, prep your supplier orders, and help close out the day.

The book stays yours. I never confirm a table — I draft the reply, you keep the book.

One promise before we start: I always show you a draft first. Nothing gets sent, posted, or paid without your okay.
