# Your AI adoption map

This map helps a restaurant add Claude work at a pace the owner can trust. Maturity belongs to each routine, not to the whole restaurant. You may stay at any stage with any routine for as long as you want.

No stage authorizes Claude to send, post, accept a reservation, order ingredients, publish a menu item, pay, promise availability, delete, or change an outside system. Use Manual mode for every new or changed routine until you review it again and approve its exact sources, output, and limits.

Only the owner may advance, pause, move back, resume, or retire a routine.

## Step 0. Readiness

Name the owner, choose one repeated job, and identify the current reservation request, confirmed special, ingredient count, menu, or closing note Claude may read. Define the local draft you want and what should stop the work. Start with one reservation reply or today's special draft. Stop if date, party details, menu item, price, availability, allergen or food-safety boundary, or responsible owner are unclear.

## 1. Assisted

Work beside Claude on one reservation reply using `skills/draft-a-reservation-reply/`, or one special using `skills/draft-todays-special/`.

- Provide the real request or confirmed special details.
- Check every date, time, party detail, item, price, and promise.
- Review the draft and receipt before you reply or post yourself.

Move only this routine forward when you have seen enough accurate work to trust its pattern.

## 2. Repeatable

Run a small manual batch of reservation replies, special drafts, review replies, ingredient-order lists, or closing notes.

- Keep each reservation and public draft separate.
- Let missing availability, menu, or party information block only that item.
- Review the final drafts and owner decisions together.

## 3. Supervised operations

Turn one proven reports-only routine into a schedule. Start with the morning brief, end-of-day close, or quiet-table fill.

Confirm the sources, run time, expected report, and pause conditions first. Every reservation, post, order, payment, refund, and customer promise remains your action.

## 4. Intent-led Business OS

Ask Claude to show which reservations, tables, ingredients, or drafts need your attention. Review one exception brief with the evidence and next decision.

Claude may surface missing reservation details, quiet periods, low ingredients, incomplete closes, or drafts waiting for review. It never decides food safety, accepts a reservation, orders ingredients, posts, or contacts anyone.

## Safe next uses

- Draft review replies from specific reviews.
- Prepare ingredient-order lists from current counts.
- Summarize the weekly menu and flag missing inputs.

## Stop or defer

- Pause if reservations, menu items, prices, counts, availability, or policies are stale or disputed.
- Defer allergen and food-safety decisions, reservation commitments, ordering, payments, refunds, public posting, customer promises, access changes, and deletion.
- Return any routine to an earlier stage whenever you want more hands-on review.
