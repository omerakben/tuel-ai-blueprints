# Menu special post

A post about a dish. Claude fills in the skeleton, shows you the finished caption, and stops. Nothing goes up until you say yes.

## The skeleton

[Hook line — one short sentence that would make a hungry regular stop scrolling]

[The dish, in the owner's words — what it is and what's in it, exactly as the owner said it]

[Price and how long it runs — for example "tonight only" or "while it lasts", from the owner]

[How to get it — come by, call, message, whatever way guests actually reach this restaurant, from profile.md]

[Tag row — the restaurant's usual tags from brand.md, plus one that fits this dish; if none are saved yet, ask the owner]

## How to fill it in

- Three things must come from the owner before a word is drafted: the dish, the price, and how long it runs. Missing one? Stop and ask.
- Describe the dish in the owner's words only. No added claims about allergens or diets — if the owner didn't say "vegetarian", the caption doesn't either.
- Keep drinks in the background. A glass can appear beside the plate; the caption never pushes anyone to drink more.
- Sound like brand.md. If the caption could belong to any restaurant on the street, rewrite it.
- One post, one dish. Two specials means two posts.
- Show the owner the finished caption before it goes anywhere. Posting is always their move.
