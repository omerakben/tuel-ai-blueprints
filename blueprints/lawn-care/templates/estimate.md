# Yard estimate

A written estimate for a yard, built from your rate sheet. Claude drafts it and shows you. Only you send it — and the final number is always yours to set after you've seen the property.

## The skeleton

Hi [first name], thanks for reaching out about [the yard — however they described it].

From what you've told me — [rough size], [condition], [what they want done] — here's where the numbers land:

- [Service]: [rate] [per visit / one time]
- [Service]: [rate] [per visit / one time]

[If it's recurring: "For [how often] visits, that's [rate] each time."]

This is an estimate from your description. I'll confirm the final price when I see the yard — no surprises, and I'll tell you before any work starts.

[How to say yes or ask a question — however clients actually reach this business, from profile.md]

— [owner or business name]

## How to fill it in

- Every rate comes from services.md or the owner's own words today. A missing rate becomes [your rate], never a guess.
- Keep the "final price confirmed at the yard" line in every estimate. It protects the owner, and clients respect it.
- No start date unless the owner confirmed one. [day] stays bracketed until they do.
- No product talk, no chemical talk, no "safe for pets by [time]" lines, and no promised results.
- Sound like brand.md. If it reads like an insurance letter, redo it.
- Show the owner the finished estimate before it goes anywhere. They send it.
