# Confirmation text

A short text confirming tomorrow's visit. Claude drafts it and shows the owner. Only the owner sends it.

## The skeleton

Hi [first name]! Planning to be at your place [day] [morning / afternoon] for [service]. [One ask, if their notes have one — "mind unlocking the side gate?" or "if the dog can stay in, we'll be quick"]. Reply here if that day doesn't work.

## How to fill it in

- Two or three sentences, no more. It's a text, not a letter.
- The day comes from a plan the owner approved or told you today. No approved day, no draft.
- The ask comes from clients.md notes, never invented. No note, no ask.
- If the owner said weather might push things, one honest line: "If rain moves us, I'll text you." Never "rain or shine".
- Only draft for clients who gave their number for texts. Not sure they did? Ask the owner first.
- It should read like the owner typed it from the truck. If it sounds like a company wrote it, redo it.
- Show the owner the exact text before it's sent. They send it — a past yes does not cover this one.
