# Route sheet

One day's stops in drive order — the sheet that rides in the truck. Claude proposes it; the owner reorders and okays it.

## The skeleton

**[Day, date] — [who's on today]**

| # | Client | Where | Service | Rough time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [name] | [street or neighborhood] | [service] | [time] | [gate side, bag or mulch, dog] |
| 2 | [name] | [street or neighborhood] | [service] | [time] | [notes] |
| 3 | [name] | [street or neighborhood] | [service] | [time] | [notes] |

**Load before leaving:** [mulch, bags, fuel — whatever this day needs]

**Still open:** [anything without a slot yet, one per line]

## How to fill it in

- The stop list comes from the plan the owner confirmed. Nobody gets added because they "seem due" — that's a question for the owner, not a row.
- The order is a proposal. The owner knows the roads, the traffic, and where the dump run fits, so they reorder freely.
- Notes come from clients.md. Never a gate code, a door code, or anything about when someone is home.
- No times promised to clients from this sheet. Telling a client a day or time is the confirmation text's job, after the owner approves it.
- Weather is the owner's call. A rainy forecast never reshuffles this sheet on its own.
