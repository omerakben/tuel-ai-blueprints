# Weekly rhythm hour

One hour a week to line up the coming week — who's due, which day each yard lands on, and the drive order for every working day. Claude proposes; you decide.

## When

Once a week, at a time you pick. Many owners use Sunday evening or Monday before the first stop. Say "let's plan the week" and Claude starts.

## Steps

1. Look back. Claude reads last week's route plan and end-of-day reports in reports/: what got done, what slid, what's still owed a visit. If there's no record yet, Claude says so and starts fresh.
2. Build the due list. From the rhythms in business/clients.md, Claude lists who's due this week. Yards without a saved rhythm go on a "need your call" list with a question — never a guess.
3. You settle the list. Cross off, add one-off jobs, push a yard to next week. Nothing gets routed until you've settled who's on.
4. Route the days. Claude runs the route playbook (skills/plan-the-routes/) to group yards by day and area and order each day into a sheet from templates/route-sheet.md.
5. You reorder. You know the roads and where the dump run fits. Claude redoes the sheets until they look right to you.
6. Offer the texts. If you want day-before confirmations, Claude drafts them with skills/draft-a-confirmation-text/ — each batch waits for your yes, and you do the sending.
7. Save the plan. The week's sheets, the "need your call" list, and anything unplaced go to reports/ as one file.

## What you get

- One route sheet per working day, in the order you approved.
- The whole week in one file, saved as reports/weekly-routes-[date].md.
- A "need your call" list and a leftover list, so nothing quietly disappears.

## Never

- This plan is internal until you act on it. No client hears a day or a time except through a confirmation you approved and sent yourself.
- Claude never guesses who's due, never invents a one-off job, and never drops a yard silently. Leftovers stay on the list.
- Weather and property access are your calls. Rain shuffles are proposed as drafts; how to get into a yard is between you and the client.
- Nothing in this hour sends, posts, books, or pays. Drafts wait for your yes, and you do the sending.
- Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
