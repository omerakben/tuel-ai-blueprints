# Your AI adoption map

This map helps a lawn care business add Claude work at a pace the owner can trust. Maturity belongs to each routine, not to the whole business. You may stay at any stage with any routine for as long as you want.

No stage authorizes Claude to send, book or route a job, promise a price or time, assign a worker, order supplies, pay, delete, or change an outside system. Use Manual mode for every new or changed routine until you review it again and approve its exact sources, output, and limits.

Only the owner may advance, pause, move back, resume, or retire a routine.

## Step 0. Readiness

Name the owner, choose one repeated job, and identify the current yard request, site notes, approved rate, confirmed job list, or invoice list Claude may read. Define the local draft you want and what should stop the work. Start with one yard estimate or confirmation draft. Stop if scope, property details, rate, route, weather or safety issue, or responsible owner are unclear.

## 1. Assisted

Work beside Claude on one estimate using `skills/draft-a-yard-estimate/`, or one confirmation using `skills/draft-a-confirmation-text/`.

- Provide the real request and current site notes.
- Check every service, property detail, amount, date, and promise.
- Review the draft and receipt before you contact or book anyone yourself.

Move only this routine forward when you have seen enough accurate work to trust its pattern.

## 2. Repeatable

Run a small manual batch of estimates, confirmations, seasonal offers, invoice reminders, or route plans.

- Keep each property and customer separate.
- Let missing scope, route, or rate information block only that item.
- Review the final drafts and owner decisions together.

## 3. Supervised operations

Turn one proven reports-only routine into a schedule. Start with the morning brief, end-of-day close, or seasonal renewal review.

Confirm the sources, run time, expected report, and pause conditions first. Every booking, route choice, assignment, message, purchase, payment, and customer promise remains your action.

## 4. Intent-led Business OS

Ask Claude to show which routes, renewals, estimates, or reminders need your attention. Review one exception brief with the evidence and next decision.

Claude may surface route conflicts, missing property details, seasonal follow-ups, aging invoices, or drafts waiting for review. It never makes a weather or safety call, books work, assigns staff, buys supplies, or contacts anyone.

## Safe next uses

- Prepare route options from a confirmed job list.
- Draft seasonal offers from owner-approved services.
- Summarize weekly routes and flag missing inputs.

## Stop or defer

- Pause if property notes, routes, rates, weather inputs, or client instructions are stale or disputed.
- Defer weather and safety decisions, chemical-use judgment, bookings, staff assignments, purchases, payments, customer promises, access changes, and deletion.
- Return any routine to an earlier stage whenever you want more hands-on review.
