# Draft a confirmation text

Tomorrow's visit confirmations — one short, personal text per client, ready for you to
send from your own phone.

## When to run this

The evening before, once you've settled tomorrow's route, or the morning of, before
you roll out. It pairs well with the end-of-day close: the day wraps up, and tomorrow's
texts are waiting before you're off the driveway.

## What Claude reads first

- The route plan in reports/ that you already approved for tomorrow, or the list you
  paste right now.
- business/clients.md — first names, the usual service, and notes like "text the night
  before" or "asked us not to text".
- business/policies.md — any limits you set on reaching out.
- business/brand.md — how you talk, so every draft sounds like you.
- templates/confirmation-text.md — the shape each text starts from.

## Steps

1. Confirm the day is real. Claude works only from a plan you approved or a list you
   just gave. If tomorrow's stops aren't settled — or weather has you unsure — Claude
   stops and asks instead of drafting texts for a day you haven't confirmed.
2. Keep only the clients whose notes in clients.md say they gave their number for
   texts. Anyone who ever asked to stop hearing from you stays off, full stop.
3. Draft one text per client from templates/confirmation-text.md. Each is two or three
   sentences, names the service, and asks for whatever you actually need — gate
   unlocked, dog inside, cars off the driveway — pulled from their notes, never invented.
4. Show you the whole batch. You edit or cut any draft, then approve the keepers by name.
5. You send them from your own phone. Claude never sends a text.
6. Save the batch to reports/ so you can tick off replies as they come in.
7. If something new came up — a client asking to stop, a new gate note — Claude
   proposes the clients.md line and waits for your yes before writing it.

## What you get

One file in reports/, a fresh one each run, named like
reports/confirmation-texts-YYYY-MM-DD.md. Inside:

- One draft text per approved client, ready to copy and send.
- The skipped list with the reason for each — no number on file, or asked to stop.
- Anything Claude couldn't settle — a stop with no rough time yet, a note that needs
  your read.

## Never

- Never send a text, and never set one up to go out later. Your phone does the sending.
- Never name a day or time you haven't confirmed. No settled plan, no texts.
- Never draft for a client who didn't give their number for texts, or who ever said stop.
- Never promise "rain or shine". If weather is iffy, the draft can say you'll text if
  plans change — the call stays yours.
- Never put a gate code, a door code, or anything like one in a text or a note.

Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
