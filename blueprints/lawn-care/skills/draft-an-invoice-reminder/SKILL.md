# Draft an invoice reminder

Friendly nudges for visits that haven't been paid yet — one per client, in your voice,
ready for you to send.

## When to run this

Whenever unpaid visits are on your mind. Many owners run it while closing out the day,
or right after the month-end tidy-up surfaces who still owes.

## What Claude reads first

- business/policies.md — your payment terms and what you do when payment runs late.
- business/clients.md — first names and notes like "always pays by check" or "asked us
  not to text".
- business/brand.md — how you talk, so a nudge still sounds like a neighbor.
- The end-of-day reports or month summary in reports/, if you want to work from those.

## Steps

1. You name who owes. Claude works only from what you say or from a report you already
   approved: the client, the visit or job, and the amount as you stated it. Claude
   never digs one up or works one out on its own.
2. Draft one reminder per client, short and warm — the first-nudge kind unless you say
   this one needs a firmer note. Each mentions the visit and the amount you gave, and
   makes paying easy in whatever way you actually take payment.
3. Flag anything odd as a question, not a fix: a client who says they already paid, an
   amount you sounded unsure of, a job that had an issue.
4. Show you the whole batch. You edit or cut any draft, then approve the keepers by name.
5. You send them yourself — text or email, your call. Claude sends nothing.
6. Save the batch to reports/ so you can tick off payments as they land.
7. If a durable fact came up — a client who always pays on the first, say — Claude
   proposes the clients.md line and waits for your yes.

## What you get

One file in reports/, a fresh one each run, named like
reports/invoice-reminders-YYYY-MM-DD.md. Inside:

- One draft reminder per approved client, ready to copy and send.
- A short note under each showing what it's based on — the visit and amount you gave.
- Questions for you, one per line — anything Claude wasn't sure about.

## Never

- Never state an amount that didn't come from you or a report you approved. No digging,
  no working it out, no "roughly".
- Never add a late fee, change terms, or threaten. What happens when payment runs late
  is your decision, made fresh — policies.md guides the words, it never fires on its own.
- Never mark anything paid or unpaid, and never touch the records. This drafts words;
  it doesn't keep books.
- Never send anything. You send every reminder personally.

Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
