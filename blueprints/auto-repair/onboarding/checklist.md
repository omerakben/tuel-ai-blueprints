## Control choices

- Name the person who approves decisions and outside actions.
- Confirm the local time zone and usual working days.
- List which files and connected tools Claude may read for routine work.
- Choose how long temporary exports in `inbox/` should be kept. Claude may flag old files but never deletes them.
- Name the accountant, lawyer, technician, or other qualified person who reviews work outside Claude's lane, when relevant.
- Choose one repeated job for the first Assisted routine. Record how it works today, its approved sources, a useful result, and a stop signal.
- Review saved evidence before advancing a routine. A stage change never authorizes sending, posting, paying, filing, signing, deciding, or deleting.

Claude works through this over time — never as a form, always as conversation. Tier 1 is enough for the first win. Everything else can wait.

## Tier 1 — needed for the first win

- Shop name
- The work that fills the week (general repair, brakes and tires, a bit of everything) and the feel of the place in three words
- Days and opening hours
- Number of bays
- Hourly labor rate and the most common jobs, with what they usually cost and rough time
- Who works the bays, what they're best at, which days — just the owner is fine
- How work comes in (calls, texts, walk-ins, a booking tool)
- How customers hear where their car stands today (calls, texts)

## Tier 2 — makes it good

- The full rate sheet (photo, file, or pasted — any shape is fine)
- House policies: the fee for looking at a car, the line above which extra work always needs a customer's okay, warranty on the shop's work, storage for cars left after pickup, how customers pay
- The regulars: names the owner wants remembered, their vehicles, how they like to hear from the shop
- Parts suppliers: who gets called first, how orders are placed, how long parts usually take
- Logo and a few photos the owner likes, dropped into `assets/`
- The shop's look and voice: three words, colors, phrases the owner loves, phrases to avoid
- Where reviews land (Google, Yelp) and whether the owner wants help replying

## Tier 3 — nice to have

- Whether the shop offers rides, loaners, or after-hours drop-off, in the owner's words
- Busiest and slowest days, as the owner sees them
- The shop's story — how it started, what it's known for
- How each technician takes their pay (hourly, flat rate), only if the owner wants the end-of-day close to track it
- Anything with a renewal date the owner wants nudges about (shop insurance, certifications, equipment checks)
