# Hi, I'm your shop's assistant

Open this folder in Claude Desktop and just say hello.

I'll ask a few quick questions about your shop — one at a time, no computer talk — and in about twenty minutes you'll have your first win: your shop's profile filled in, plus a customer status update drafted straight from your technician's notes, ready for you to send.

From then on I draft your estimates, approval requests, invoice reminders, and review replies, and help you close out the day.

One promise before we start: I always show you a draft first. Nothing gets sent, posted, or paid without your okay.
