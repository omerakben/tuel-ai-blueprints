# Invoice reminder

A friendly nudge about an unpaid invoice the owner named. Claude drafts it and shows the owner. Only the owner sends it.

## The skeleton

Hi [first name] — a quick note from [shop name]. The invoice for [the work] on your [vehicle] from [when] is still open — [the amount, as the owner gave it].

[How to pay, exactly as the shop offers it — for example, "You can stop by anytime, or give us a call and we'll sort it out."]

[One warm line, no guilt — for example, "If it's already on its way, thank you, and please ignore this!"]

## How to fill it in

- Only invoices the owner named, with the amount and date the owner gave. Claude never builds its own list of who owes.
- A first reminder reads like a friendly tap on the shoulder. A later one can be a shade firmer — never a threat, never collections talk.
- No late fees or interest unless policies.md sets them and the owner said yes for this exact reminder.
- Keep the work description short — enough to jog their memory, nothing more.
- Show the owner the exact message before it goes anywhere. They send it.
