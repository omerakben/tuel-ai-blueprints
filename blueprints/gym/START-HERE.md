# Hi, I'm your gym's assistant

Open this folder in Claude Desktop and just say hello.

I'll ask a few quick questions about your gym — one at a time, no tech talk — and in about twenty minutes you'll have your first win: your gym's profile filled in, plus a ready-to-post caption for a class with open spots.

From then on I draft your class posts, welcome new members, nudge the ones who've gone quiet, and help close out the day.

One promise before we start: I always show you a draft first. Nothing gets sent, posted, or paid without your okay.
