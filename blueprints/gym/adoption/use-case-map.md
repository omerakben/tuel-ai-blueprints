# Your AI adoption map

This map helps a gym or fitness studio add Claude work at a pace the owner can trust. Maturity belongs to each routine, not to the whole studio. You may stay at any stage with any routine for as long as you want.

No stage authorizes Claude to send, post, enroll or remove a member, change a class, charge or refund, make an employment decision, delete, or change an outside system. Use Manual mode for every new or changed routine until you review it again and approve its exact sources, output, and limits.

Only the owner may advance, pause, move back, resume, or retire a routine.

## Step 0. Readiness

Name the owner, choose one repeated admin job, and identify the current class list, member contact preference, approved offer, or review Claude may read. Define the local draft you want and what should stop the work. Start with one welcome note or class-fill post. Stop if consent, class facts, price, offer, audience, health information, or responsible owner are unclear.

## 1. Assisted

Work beside Claude on one welcome note using `skills/draft-a-welcome-note/`, or one class-fill post using `skills/draft-a-class-fill-post/`.

- Provide confirmed class or membership facts only.
- Check every date, time, offer, audience, and promise.
- Review the draft and receipt before you contact anyone or post yourself.

Move only this routine forward when you have seen enough accurate work to trust its pattern.

## 2. Repeatable

Run a small manual batch of welcome notes, renewal notes, class-fill posts, lapsed-member drafts, or review replies.

- Keep each member and public draft separate.
- Let missing consent, contact preference, or class information block only that item.
- Review the final drafts and owner decisions together.

## 3. Supervised operations

Turn one proven reports-only routine into a schedule. Start with the morning brief, end-of-day close, or class-fill push.

Confirm the sources, run time, expected report, and pause conditions first. Every message, post, membership change, schedule change, charge, refund, and customer promise remains your action.

## 4. Intent-led Business OS

Ask Claude to show which classes, renewals, or drafts need your attention. Review one exception brief with the evidence and next decision.

Claude may surface underfilled classes, renewal drafts, missing contact preferences, or replies waiting for review. It never gives exercise or health guidance, evaluates a person, changes membership, posts, or contacts anyone.

## Safe next uses

- Draft renewal notes from owner-confirmed membership dates.
- Draft review replies from specific reviews.
- Summarize the weekly schedule and flag missing inputs.

## Stop or defer

- Pause if class details, consent, offers, membership status, or contact preferences are stale or disputed.
- Defer health and safety guidance, eligibility or employment decisions, membership changes, charges, refunds, public posting, access changes, and deletion.
- Return any routine to an earlier stage whenever you want more hands-on review.
