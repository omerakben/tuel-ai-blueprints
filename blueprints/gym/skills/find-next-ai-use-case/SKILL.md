# Find the next AI use case

Use this playbook to choose one small-business routine that Claude can safely observe or draft.

## Inputs

- The owner's current bottlenecks and priorities.
- Recent operations notes and work receipts.
- Approved business facts and available source files.
- The time, delay, rework, or missed-item baseline when known.

## Steps

1. Read the full `CLAUDE.md` and `ADOPT-AI.md`.
2. Ask which repeated work consumes attention, waits too long, or is often missed.
3. List only candidates that are frequent, evidence-based, easy to review, reversible, and useful as a local observation or draft.
4. Exclude work that depends on restricted information, outside action, licensed judgment, destructive changes, or an owner decision Claude should not make.
5. Compare up to three candidates by expected value, source readiness, review effort, risk, and ease of measuring improvement.
6. Recommend one candidate and prepare `templates/ai-use-case-card.md` for it. Mark every missing fact and use Manual permission for the first run.
7. Save the draft card and a work receipt to `reports/`. The owner decides whether to start the routine.

Do not enable a schedule, widen access, change permission mode, or advance an adoption stage.

Action level: B - drafts only.
