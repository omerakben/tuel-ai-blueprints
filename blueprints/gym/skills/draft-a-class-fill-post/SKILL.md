# Fill a class

Turn a class with open spots into a ready-to-post caption. Claude drafts it; you post it.

## When to run this

- Tomorrow's spin class has four open bikes and you'd like them full.
- A new time slot just went on the timetable and nobody knows yet.
- A regular cancelled and a good spot opened up at short notice.
- The evening class-fill check (schedules/class-fill-push.md) flagged a light class and you want the post now.

## What Claude reads first

- business/services.md — the class name, price, and what it includes, so nothing gets made up.
- business/profile.md — your hours and how people book a spot.
- business/staff.md — to name the coach, if you want them mentioned.
- business/brand.md — how you talk, so the post sounds like you.
- templates/class-post.md — the shape the post starts from.

Claude also needs three things from you: which class, the day and time, and how many spots are open. If any of the three is missing, Claude stops and asks. It never guesses at your timetable.

## Steps

1. Tell Claude the class, the day and time, and the spots open. Missing one of the three? Claude stops and asks before writing a word.
2. Claude checks the class against business/services.md. If the class is not there, Claude asks what it costs and what it includes rather than inventing it — and offers to save your answer for next time, shown as exact lines first.
3. Claude drafts one post from templates/class-post.md: what the class is, when it runs, that there is space, and how to grab a spot. Your voice from brand.md, your usual tags if you have saved any.
4. Claude shows you the draft and waits. Ask for changes as many times as you like — shorter, warmer, a different angle. Nothing moves until you say it is good.
5. With your yes, Claude saves the post to reports/. You copy it into Instagram, Facebook, or wherever your members are, and post it yourself.
6. If the class fills or the details change, tell Claude and it drafts a fresh version. The old draft stays in reports/ as a record.

## What you get

A ready-to-post caption saved as reports/class-fill-post-YYYY-MM-DD.md — the class, the time, the spots open, and the approved post, ready to copy.

## Never

- Never draft without the class, the time, and the spots-open count. If one is missing, Claude stops and asks — it never pads around a gap.
- Never invent a price, a discount, a start time, or a coach. If it is not in business/ or something you just said, Claude asks.
- Never promise results in a post. No weight-loss claims, no transformation talk — "come feel welcome, all levels" beats "come lose weight" every time.
- Never use a member's photo, name, or words unless you confirm that member said yes — and for anyone under eighteen, a parent or guardian's yes as well.
- Never post anything anywhere. The draft waits in reports/ until you post it yourself.

Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
