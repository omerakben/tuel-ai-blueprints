# Reply to a review

You paste in a review from Google or wherever it landed, and Claude drafts a reply in your gym's own voice for you to post.

## When to run this

- A new review just landed.
- A rough review came in and you would rather see a calm draft than type one while upset.
- An older review has sat unanswered and it bothers you.
- You want your replies to sound like one voice, whatever kind of day it has been.

## What Claude reads first

- business/brand.md — how your gym talks, so the reply sounds like you and not like a form letter.
- business/staff.md — to name the right coach when the review mentions one.
- business/services.md — to get class and membership names right.
- business/policies.md — your hard limits, in case the review touches on freezes, cancellations, or refunds.
- templates/review-reply.md — the shape each reply starts from.

You paste or say: the full review text, where it came from, the star rating if there is one, and anything you remember. If a detail is missing, Claude asks you rather than guessing.

Claude never needs your Google password. It works only with the text you paste.

## Steps

1. Paste the review and tell Claude where it came from.
2. Claude reads it and takes its temperature. If anything is unclear — who coached them, which class it was — Claude asks you before writing a word.
3. For a happy review, Claude drafts a short thank-you that mentions the class or coach the reviewer praised, using only names that match your business files, and says the door is always open.
4. For an unhappy or mixed review, Claude drafts a calm reply that thanks them for telling you and moves the conversation off the page: the draft asks them to reach the gym at [your preferred contact] so you can talk it through directly. It does not argue and it does not make excuses. It never offers a refund, a free month, or anything else to make it up, unless you decide that yourself. If you do, tell Claude your exact offer and it goes into the draft word for word.
5. Claude shows you the draft and waits. Ask for changes as many times as you like — shorter, warmer. Nothing moves until you say the draft is good.
6. You post the reply yourself. Claude never posts it for you.
7. Claude saves a copy to reports/ so you have a record of what was said.
8. If the review points to something worth remembering — a third complaint about the changing rooms — Claude proposes a note for your business files and writes it only if you agree.

## What you get

A ready-to-post reply, saved as reports/review-reply-YYYY-MM-DD.md, one file per review. The file holds the original review, where it came from, the reply you approved, and whether you posted it — so the next time a similar review lands, Claude can match the tone you liked.

## Never

- Never draft a fake review, and never write anything meant to look like it came from a member.
- Never offer a refund, a free month, or a free class in a reply unless you decided that for this exact review.
- Never suggest paying or rewarding anyone for a review, and never offer anything in exchange for changing or removing one.
- Never confirm in public that the reviewer is a member, when they trained, or anything else about them — that stays between you and them.
- Never answer a complaint about an injury or a health scare with advice about it. The reply stays kind, invites them to contact the gym, and leaves anything medical to a professional.
- Never promise results in a reply, even to soften a complaint — no "you'll see progress if you stick with it".

Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
