# Win back a quiet member

You name who has gone quiet; Claude drafts one warm, no-guilt note per person. You decide who hears from you, and you send it.

## When to run this

- A regular has not been in for a while and you miss them.
- A few names have drifted and you want to reach out before the gap gets awkward.
- A quiet month has you thinking about the people behind the numbers.

## What Claude reads first

- business/clients.md — their usual class and anything you've saved, so each note lands personal.
- business/brand.md — how you talk, so "we miss you" sounds like you and not like a campaign.
- business/policies.md — your always-ask list.
- templates/win-back-note.md — the shape each note starts from.

The names come from you. Claude never combs through anything to decide who counts as lapsed — you know your people, and you name them.

## Steps

1. Tell Claude who has gone quiet — one name or a short list.
2. For each person, Claude checks clients.md for their usual: the class they came to, the name they go by. If there is nothing saved, Claude asks you for one true detail rather than writing a generic note.
3. Claude drafts one note per person from templates/win-back-note.md: they're missed, the door is open, and one true current thing worth coming back for — a class running this week, a new time slot — only if you shared it. No guilt, no streak talk, no "we noticed you stopped".
4. Every note carries an easy out, a plain line like "and if the timing's not right just now, no worries at all — we're here when you're ready".
5. Claude shows you the whole batch. You edit or cut any note, then approve the keepers by name. Anyone you cross off stays off.
6. You send each note yourself, from your own phone or email. Claude saves the batch to reports/.
7. If someone replies that they'd rather not hear from the gym, tell Claude. It proposes the line for clients.md so they stay off every future list, and writes it after your yes.

## What you get

One file in reports/, a fresh one each run, named reports/win-back-notes-YYYY-MM-DD.md: the names you gave, one approved note per person ready to copy, and a skipped list with the reason for each — crossed off by you, or asked not to be contacted.

## Never

- Never decide who counts as lapsed. The owner names names; Claude only writes notes.
- Never guilt anyone. No "it's been six weeks", no lost-progress talk, no digs at the gap — kindly meant or not.
- Never mention weight, fitness, health, or an injury. If the owner says an injury is why someone stopped, the note skips it entirely — "hope you're doing well" is the limit, and anything medical belongs with their doctor or physio.
- Never write to anyone who asked to stop hearing from the gym, no matter how long they've been away.
- Never send anything. The notes wait in reports/ until you send them yourself.

Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
