# Renewal notes

You say whose membership is coming due; Claude drafts a personal note for each. You send the notes, and only you touch the memberships.

## When to run this

- A handful of memberships come due this month and you want to reach out before they lapse.
- Someone's class pack is nearly used up and you'd like to check in warmly.
- You'd rather a personal note reached them before the payment date does.

## What Claude reads first

- business/clients.md — each member's usual class and rhythm, so the note feels personal.
- business/services.md — what the membership or pack includes, at the price you set.
- business/policies.md — your rules on renewals and freezes, and your always-ask list.
- business/brand.md — how you talk.
- templates/renewal-note.md — the shape each note starts from.

The list of who is coming due comes from you — say it, paste it, or read it out of your membership tool. Claude never assumes a renewal date it was not given.

## Steps

1. Tell Claude who is coming due and when. Names and dates come from you. If a date is fuzzy — "sometime next month?" — Claude asks rather than picking one.
2. For each person, Claude checks clients.md for their usual: the class they love, the time they always come. That is what makes the note read like you wrote it, not like a bill.
3. Claude drafts one note per person from templates/renewal-note.md. Each is short, warm, mentions what they'd keep — their usual spot, their usual class — and asks if they'd like to keep going. No pressure lines, no countdowns.
4. Claude shows you the whole batch. You edit or cut any note, then approve the keepers by name. A yes to one is not a yes to all.
5. You send each note yourself — text, email, or a word at the front desk. Claude saves the batch to reports/ so you can tick off replies as they come in.
6. Whatever each member decides, the membership itself is yours to handle. You renew it, charge it, pause it, or let it go, in your own tool, with your own hands. Claude's part ended at the words.
7. If someone's answer changes what you know — a new usual class, a request to stop hearing from you — Claude proposes the line for clients.md and writes it only after your yes.

## What you get

One file in reports/, a fresh one each run, named reports/renewal-notes-YYYY-MM-DD.md: who is coming due and when, one approved note per person ready to copy, and a "need your call" list for anyone Claude could not write for without asking you something first.

## Never

- Never renew, charge, extend, freeze, or change anyone's membership or its terms. Claude drafts words; the membership is yours alone.
- Never invent a renewal date, a price, or what a membership includes. A missing fact gets a question, not a guess.
- Never lean on guilt or fear. "We'd love to keep seeing you" beats "don't lose your progress" — no countdowns, no streak talk.
- Never mention a member's attendance record, body, fitness, or results in a note. The note is about keeping their spot, not measuring them.
- Never send anything. The notes wait in reports/ until you send them yourself.

Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
