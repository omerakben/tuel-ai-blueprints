# Weekly rhythm hour

One hour a week to look at the week's classes and get ahead of it. Claude drafts the posts and notes; you decide what goes out.

## When

Once a week, at a time you pick. Many owners use Sunday evening or the quiet stretch after the Monday morning rush. Say "let's do the week" and Claude starts.

## Steps

1. Look back. Claude reads last week's plan in reports/ and checks what went out and what worked. If there is no record yet, Claude says so and starts fresh.
2. Lay out the week. You share this week's class list and bookings — paste it, read it out, or drop it in assets/. Claude maps the week: which classes look full, which have space, who coaches what. No list means no guessing — Claude asks.
3. Pick the priorities together. Claude suggests where the hour is best spent — a couple of classes that need filling, a renewal batch coming due, a quiet regular worth a note — and says why. You choose, or name your own.
4. Draft the fill posts. For each class you picked, Claude drafts a post using templates/class-post.md, in your voice from business/brand.md. The class, the time, and the spots come from the list you shared, never from memory.
5. Draft the notes. If you named renewals coming due or members gone quiet, Claude drafts those with the right playbook — one note per person, no one added without your say.
6. Approve the batch. Claude lays everything out in one place — every post and every note, together. You go item by item: say yes or no, or tell Claude what to change. Changed items come back for another look. An item you skip is a no. Only items with your fresh yes make the plan.
7. Make the week's plan. Claude ends with a short plan: each approved item, the day and rough time you will post or send it, and where it goes. You do the posting and sending. The plan and the approved drafts are saved to reports/.

## What you get

- Fill posts for the classes you chose, plus any renewal or win-back notes — approved by you and ready to copy.
- A one-page plan for the week saved as reports/weekly-schedule-[date].md, with the approved drafts underneath it.

## Never

- Claude never posts or sends anything. Not one word leaves this folder without you.
- Claude never invents a class, a time, a price, or an offer. If it is not in your business/ files, the list you shared, or something you just said, Claude asks.
- A yes covers that exact item, that week. Last week's yes approves nothing this week, and staying quiet on an item is a no.
- No promised results and no body talk in any post or note. "All levels welcome" is the house style.
- Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
