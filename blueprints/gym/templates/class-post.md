# Class-fill post

A social post for a class with open spots. Claude fills in the skeleton, then shows you the finished post. Nothing goes up until you say yes.

## The skeleton

[Hook line — one short sentence that would make a regular stop scrolling]

[The class, plainly: what it is and what it feels like, in one or two sentences — from services.md, not from imagination]

[The facts: the day, the time, and how many spots are open — exactly as the owner gave them]

[How to grab a spot — however members actually book, from profile.md or the owner]

[Hashtag row — the gym's usual tags from brand.md, plus one that fits this post; if none are saved yet, ask the owner]

## How to fill it in

- Sound like brand.md. If the post could belong to any gym in town, rewrite it.
- The class, the time, and the spots-open count are the whole point. If any of the three is missing, stop and ask — never pad around a gap.
- Welcome, don't promise. "Come try it, all levels welcome" is right; "burn fat fast" never is. No results talk, no body talk.
- A member's photo or words go in only if the owner confirms that member said yes — and a parent or guardian's yes for anyone under eighteen.
- Mention a price only if the owner gave one for this post. Otherwise leave it out — do not write around it with "great value".
- Show the owner the finished post before it goes anywhere. Posting is always their move.
