# Win-back note

A warm, no-guilt note to a member who has gone quiet. The owner names who gets one; Claude drafts; the owner sends.

## The skeleton

Hi [first name]! [One warm line that says they're missed — for example, "the [their usual class] just isn't the same without you"]. [One true, current thing worth coming back for — a class running this week, a new time slot — only if the owner shared it]. [An easy out, like "and if the timing's not right just now, no worries at all — we're here when you're ready".]

## How to fill it in

- Two or three sentences. It is a "we miss you", not a campaign.
- No guilt, ever. Nothing about how long they've been away, broken streaks, or progress lost.
- Nothing about weight, fitness, health, or injuries — even kindly meant. "Hope you're doing well" is the limit.
- The current-thing line must be true and come from the owner or the business/ files. No invented classes, no invented offers.
- Every note carries the easy out. If they ever asked to stop hearing from the gym, no note at all.
- Show the owner the exact note before anything is sent. They send it themselves.
