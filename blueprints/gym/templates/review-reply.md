# Review reply

A reply to a public review, in the gym's voice. Claude drafts it; the owner posts it.

## The skeleton

For a happy review:

[Thank them by name if the review shows one.] [One specific line back — the class or coach they praised, names checked against the business files.] [A warm "see you soon".]

For an unhappy or mixed review:

[Thank them for telling you — no arguing, no excuses.] [One calm line that takes the concern seriously without confirming private details.] [Move it off the page: "we'd really like to talk this through — reach us at [owner's preferred contact]".]

## How to fill it in

- Three sentences is usually enough. A public reply is read by everyone, not just the reviewer.
- Match the tone in brand.md, on good days and bad ones.
- Never confirm in public that someone is a member or when they trained. That stays private.
- No refunds, free months, or make-goods unless the owner decided that for this exact review — then their exact words, word for word.
- If the review mentions an injury or a health worry, the reply stays kind and general and invites them to get in touch. No advice about the injury itself.
- Show the owner the exact reply before it goes anywhere. They post it; Claude never does.
