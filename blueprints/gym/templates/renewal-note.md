# Renewal note

A short, personal note to a member whose membership or class pack is coming due. Claude drafts it and shows the owner. Only the owner sends it — and only the owner touches the membership itself.

## The skeleton

Hi [first name]! [One warm, true line about them — for example, "the [their usual class] crew isn't the same without you on Tuesdays"]. Your [membership or pack] comes up on [date the owner gave]. Want to keep your spot going? [How to renew, in the owner's words — "just reply here" or "grab me at the desk".]

## How to fill it in

- Three or four sentences, no more. It is a note from a person, not a billing reminder.
- The renewal date comes from the owner, never from a guess. No date, no draft — ask.
- Warm and zero pressure. No countdowns, no "don't lose your progress", no guilt.
- Their usual class or time comes from clients.md or the owner. Not sure what their usual is? Ask.
- Nothing about attendance, body, or results. The note is about keeping their spot, not measuring them.
- Show the owner the exact note before anything is sent. They send it; Claude never does, and Claude never renews or charges anything.
