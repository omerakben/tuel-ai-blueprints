# Welcome note

A warm first note to a brand-new member. Claude drafts it and shows the owner. The owner sends it.

## The skeleton

Hi [first name], so glad you're in! [One line about what they joined — for example, "see you at [their first class or usual time]"]. A couple of things for week one: [one or two first-week pointers from services.md — which class is a good start, what to bring, where to park]. Any questions at all, [how to reach the owner — "just reply here" or whatever fits]. See you soon!

## How to fill it in

- Short and human — a welcome, not a handbook. Four or five sentences at most.
- The first-week pointers come from services.md. If that section is still blank, ask the owner for one or two real ones — never pad with generic advice.
- No workout plans, no diet tips, no promised results. Week one is about feeling at home.
- Use the name they go by, from the owner.
- Show the owner the exact note before anything is sent. They send it from their own phone or email.
