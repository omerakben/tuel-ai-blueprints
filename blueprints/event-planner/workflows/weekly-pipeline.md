# Weekly pipeline hour

One hour a week to walk the whole pipeline — every inquiry, every proposal out, every booked event — with follow-up drafts ready and you deciding what goes out.

## When

Once a week, at a time you pick. Many owners take Monday morning, before the week starts pulling. Say "let's do the pipeline hour" and Claude starts.

## Steps

1. Gather the board. Claude reads business/clients.md and the recent files in reports/ — inquiry replies, proposals, the lead sweep — and lays out one line per lead and per event: who, what, where it stands, and who's waiting on whom.
2. Check the quiet ones. Inquiries that have sat for a week or more get flagged. For each, Claude proposes a short follow-up draft, or a "let it rest" if you say the lead has gone cold. Marking a lead lost is your call, never Claude's.
3. Walk the booked events. For each one, Claude checks its timeline in reports/ if there is one: what's due this week, which vendors owe you replies, and which deposit dates are close on your list. Anything outward — a vendor nudge, a client update, a reminder — becomes a draft.
4. Decide item by item. Claude lays every draft out in one place and you go through them: yes, no, or change it. Changed items come back for another look. An item you skip is a no.
5. Set the week. Claude ends with a short plan: what you'll send and when, what you're waiting on, and what to check next week.
6. Save it. The board, the approved drafts, and the plan are saved as reports/weekly-pipeline-[date].md.

## What you get

- The pipeline on one page: every lead and every event, with where each stands.
- Follow-up and nudge drafts, each approved by you and ready to copy.
- A short plan for the week, saved with the drafts in reports/weekly-pipeline-[date].md.

## Never

- Claude never sends a follow-up, a nudge, or an update. Every message leaves from your hands.
- Claude never marks a lead lost, a vendor confirmed, or a date settled on its own — those calls are yours.
- Claude never treats a vendor or venue as available unless you said you confirmed it, and it never books or holds anything.
- A yes covers that exact item, this week. Last week's yes approves nothing today, and staying quiet on an item is a no.
- Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
