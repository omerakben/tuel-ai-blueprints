# Deposit reminder

Friendly nudges for deposits coming due, drafted from your list — you read, you send, and the money stays entirely in your hands.

## When to run this

- On your invoicing rhythm — many owners run it weekly, alongside the pipeline hour.
- When a deposit or balance date is coming up and you want the note ready early.
- Any time you'd rather not write "just checking in about the payment" from scratch again.

## What Claude reads first

- Your list of what's due — paste it or say it plainly: who, which payment, and the date. This playbook runs on your list, nothing else.
- business/clients.md — the event behind each name, so every note sounds personal.
- business/policies.md — your deposit and payment rhythm, so the reminder matches what the client already agreed to.
- business/brand.md — your voice.
- templates/deposit-reminder.md — the shape each note starts from.

## Steps

1. Share the list. Claude works only from the deposits and dates you give it — it never scans around for who might owe what, and it never computes an amount you didn't state.
2. Claude checks each name against business/clients.md and asks about anything that doesn't line up — a date that moved, an event that changed — instead of guessing.
3. Claude drafts one short note per client from templates/deposit-reminder.md: a warm line about their event, the payment and its date in plain words, and how to pay in your words. No two read the same.
4. Anything unclear becomes a question for you, not a line in a draft: "you said the [event] balance moved — is the new date settled?"
5. Claude shows you the whole batch and waits. You edit or cut any note, then approve the keepers by name. You send each one yourself — a yes to the words never means Claude sends them.
6. The batch is saved to reports/ so you can tick off replies as they come in.
7. If a date or plan changed along the way, Claude offers the exact updated lines for business/clients.md and writes them only after your yes.

## What you get

One file in reports/, a fresh one each run, named like reports/deposit-reminder-YYYY-MM-DD.md:

- The list you gave, with each item's status: drafted, needs your call, or skipped.
- One ready-to-send note per approved client.
- Questions for you, one per line, wherever something didn't line up.

## Never

- Never send a reminder, and never set one up to go out later. Your hands, every note, every time.
- Never take, chase, or promise money. This playbook writes words; the payment itself is between you and the client, in your hands only.
- Never invent or adjust an amount or a due date. Your list is the only source.
- Never add pressure you didn't ask for — no late fees, no warnings, no "final notice" unless those words are yours.
- Never share one client's payment details with another client, or with any vendor.

Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
