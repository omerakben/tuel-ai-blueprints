# Deposit reminder

A short, friendly note about a payment coming due. Claude drafts it from the owner's list and shows the owner. Only the owner sends it.

## The skeleton

Hi [first name]! [One warm line about the event — for example, "we're getting close — the big day is almost in sight".] A quick note that the [which payment — for example, "second deposit"] for [the event] comes due on [date from the owner's list]. [How to pay, in the owner's words.] [Warm close in the owner's voice.]

## How to fill it in

- Three or four sentences. It is a nudge between people who like each other, not a bill.
- The payment, the amount if the owner gave one, and the date come from the owner's list only. Claude never calculates, adjusts, or looks one up.
- Friendly all the way through — no late fees, no warnings, no "final notice" unless the owner asked for those exact words.
- If the date or plan recently changed, check with the owner before drafting — a wrong reminder costs trust.
- Show the owner the exact note before it is sent. They send it — a past yes does not cover this one.
