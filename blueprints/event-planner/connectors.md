# Connect your tools (all optional)

Your first win needs none of this. Claude works from this folder and what you tell it. Connect things later, and only if they save you time.

Every connection starts read-only: Claude can look things up, but it changes nothing without you.

## Gmail

When your Gmail is connected, inquiry replies and client notes land in your drafts, ready to send. You still press send, every time.

## Square (or whatever takes your payments)

When the tool that takes your payments is connected, the month-end tidy-up can check what came in against your own list instead of you reading numbers out. You review the summary before it goes anywhere, and every charge and refund stays in your hands.

## Google Business Profile

If your business listing can be connected, Claude helps you keep hours and services current and prepares your review replies, ready for you to paste. Posting is always yours.

## Instagram and Facebook

No connection needed here at all. Claude writes the post and the caption; you copy it into Instagram or Facebook when you're happy with it. Posting is always yours.

---

A mention here does not switch anything on. If you want one of these, say so and Claude walks you through it. You can turn any of them off whenever you like.
