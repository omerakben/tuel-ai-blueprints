# Your AI adoption map

This map helps an event planner add Claude work at a pace the owner can trust. Maturity belongs to each routine, not to the whole business. You may stay at any stage with any routine for as long as you want.

No stage authorizes Claude to send, accept an event, book a vendor, promise a date or price, collect a deposit, pay, sign, delete, or change an outside system. Use Manual mode for every new or changed routine until you review it again and approve its exact sources, output, and limits.

Only the owner may advance, pause, move back, resume, or retire a routine.

## Step 0. Readiness

Name the owner, choose one repeated job, and identify the current inquiry, approved service list, event brief, vendor note, or payment status Claude may read. Define the local draft you want and what should stop the work. Start with one inquiry reply or event proposal. Stop if date, scope, budget, availability, contract term, safety issue, or responsible owner are unclear.

## 1. Assisted

Work beside Claude on one inquiry reply using `skills/draft-an-inquiry-reply/`, or one proposal using `skills/draft-an-event-proposal/`.

- Provide the real inquiry and confirmed business terms.
- Check every date, service, amount, vendor reference, and promise.
- Review the draft and receipt before you contact or commit to anyone yourself.

Move only this routine forward when you have seen enough accurate work to trust its pattern.

## 2. Repeatable

Run a small manual batch of inquiry replies, proposals, timelines, vendor briefs, or deposit reminders.

- Keep each event and client separate.
- Let a missing date, term, or vendor answer block only that item.
- Review the final drafts and owner decisions together.

## 3. Supervised operations

Turn one proven reports-only routine into a schedule. Start with the morning brief, end-of-day close, or lead sweep.

Confirm the sources, run time, expected report, and pause conditions first. Every commitment, booking, message, deposit, payment, signature, and customer promise remains your action.

## 4. Intent-led Business OS

Ask Claude to show which leads, milestones, vendors, deposits, or drafts need your attention. Review one exception brief with the evidence and next decision.

Claude may surface unanswered inquiries, missing event details, late decisions, vendor gaps, or drafts waiting for review. It never books, commits, signs, collects money, pays, or contacts anyone.

## Safe next uses

- Prepare planning timelines from confirmed milestones.
- Draft vendor briefs from an approved event plan.
- Summarize the weekly pipeline and flag missing inputs.

## Stop or defer

- Pause if dates, terms, budgets, vendor status, or client instructions are stale or disputed.
- Defer contract judgment, safety decisions, bookings, deposits, payments, signatures, customer promises, access changes, and deletion.
- Return any routine to an earlier stage whenever you want more hands-on review.
