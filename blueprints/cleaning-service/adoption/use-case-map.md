# Your AI adoption map

This map helps a cleaning service add Claude work at a pace the owner can trust. Maturity belongs to each routine, not to the whole business. You may stay at any stage with any routine for as long as you want.

No stage authorizes Claude to send, book a job, assign a worker, promise a price or time, buy supplies, pay, delete, or change an outside system. Use Manual mode for every new or changed routine until you review it again and approve its exact sources, output, and limits.

Only the owner may advance, pause, move back, resume, or retire a routine.

## Step 0. Readiness

Name the owner, choose one repeated job, and identify the current service request, confirmed job list, route notes, or invoice list Claude may read. Define the local draft you want and what should stop the work. Start with one confirmation text or job estimate. Stop if scope, location, rate, access instruction, safety issue, or responsible owner are unclear.

## 1. Assisted

Work beside Claude on one confirmation draft using `skills/draft-a-confirmation-text/`, or one estimate using `skills/draft-a-job-estimate/`.

- Provide the real request and approved service details.
- Check every address detail, service, price, date, and promise.
- Review the draft and receipt before you contact or book anyone yourself.

Move only this routine forward when you have seen enough accurate work to trust its pattern.

## 2. Repeatable

Run a small manual batch of confirmations, estimates, recurring-service offers, invoice reminders, or route plans.

- Keep each client and job separate.
- Let a missing scope, rate, or access detail block only that item.
- Review the final drafts and owner decisions together.

## 3. Supervised operations

Turn one proven reports-only routine into a schedule. Start with the morning brief, end-of-day close, or open-slot fill.

Confirm the sources, run time, expected report, and pause conditions first. Every booking, assignment, message, purchase, payment, and customer promise remains your action.

## 4. Intent-led Business OS

Ask Claude to show which routes, open slots, estimates, or reminders need your attention. Review one exception brief with the evidence and next decision.

Claude may surface route conflicts, missing job details, open time, aging estimates, or drafts waiting for review. It never books, assigns staff, buys supplies, or contacts anyone.

## Safe next uses

- Draft recurring-service offers from owner-approved terms.
- Prepare route options from a confirmed job list.
- Summarize weekly routes and flag missing inputs.

## Stop or defer

- Pause if job details, rates, routes, access notes, or client instructions are stale or disputed.
- Defer safety decisions, staff assignments, bookings, purchases, payments, refunds, customer promises, access changes, and deletion.
- Return any routine to an earlier stage whenever you want more hands-on review.
