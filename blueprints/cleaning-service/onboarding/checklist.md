# What to collect, in order

## Control choices

- Name the person who approves decisions and outside actions.
- Confirm the local time zone and usual working days.
- List which files and connected tools Claude may read for routine work.
- Choose how long temporary exports in `inbox/` should be kept. Claude may flag old files but never deletes them.
- Name the accountant, lawyer, technician, or other qualified person who reviews work outside Claude's lane, when relevant.

Claude works through this over time — never as a form, always as conversation. Tier 1 is enough for the first win. Everything else can wait.

## Tier 1 — needed for the first win

- Business name
- Homes, offices, or both — and the three words for how it should sound
- Days and working hours
- Who does the cleaning — just the owner is a fine answer; names, strengths, and days if there's a crew
- Top services with what they usually run and rough duration
- How jobs get priced (flat by size, by the hour, walk-through first)
- How new clients reach the business
- Jobs the owner turns down (mold, sewage, anything hazardous), and who they refer to if anyone

## Tier 2 — makes it good

- The full rate sheet (photo, file, or pasted — any shape is fine)
- House rules: cancellations, rescheduling, what a standard clean includes, and how keys are handled — as a policy in general words, never as codes or key locations
- The regulars: names the owner wants remembered, home or office, their usual clean, how often, the day they prefer, the part of town, pets, products they prefer
- Who's waiting for a slot — clients who asked for more visits, a different day, or a call when something opens
- Suppliers: where products and paper goods come from, and how orders get placed
- Logo and a few photos of the work the owner likes, dropped into `assets/`
- The business's voice: phrases the owner loves, phrases to avoid
- Where reviews land (Google, Yelp) and whether the owner wants help replying

## Tier 3 — nice to have

- Supply restock points: how low things get before it's time to buy more
- Busiest and slowest days, as the owner sees them
- The business's story — how it started, what it's known for
- How each crew member is paid, only if the owner wants the end-of-day close to track it
- Anything with a renewal date the owner wants nudges about (insurance, bonding)
