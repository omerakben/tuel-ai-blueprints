# Recurring-clean offer

The "want us back on a regular day?" note after a one-time clean that went well. Claude drafts it; the owner sends it.

## The skeleton

Hi [first name]! Really enjoyed getting your [home/office] in shape [today / on [day]]. If you'd like it to stay that way without thinking about it, we could come by [the rhythm the owner offers, like every two weeks] — [what a regular visit runs, only if the owner gave a number]. No pressure at all — just reply if you'd like a regular spot and we'll find a day that suits you. — [owner or business name]

## How to fill it in

- Warm and short, sent while the clean is still fresh in their mind. The same day is best.
- Mention something true about the job just done. A note that could have gone to anyone reads like it went to no one.
- The rhythm and the rate come from the owner or services.md. If neither says, ask — never invent a rate or a discount.
- One easy out, always. If they say no or say nothing, that's the end of it.
- Show the owner the exact note before it's sent. Sending is their move.
