# Job checklist

A one-page checklist for a single job — what to do, what to bring, what to note for next time. Claude fills it in from the client's page; the owner or the crew ticks it off.

## The skeleton

**Client:** [name or nickname]
**Day and window:** [from the route plan or tomorrow's list]
**Home or office:** [which]

**The clean**

- [ ] [room or task, one per line — from the client's usual in clients.md plus anything asked for this visit]
- [ ] [room or task]
- [ ] [extras this visit, if any — like inside the oven]

**Bring**

- [ ] [supplies or products this client prefers, by product name from clients.md]

**Notes for next visit**

- [what the crew noticed — running low on their preferred product, a room that needs a deeper pass next time]

## How to fill it in

- The task list comes from the client's usual clean in clients.md plus what was agreed for this visit. Nothing gets added because it "seems standard" — if it's unclear, ask.
- Products are listed by name only, as the owner uses them. No mixing or combining instructions, ever — the label is the only authority on how a product is used.
- No door codes, alarm codes, key locations, or entry instructions on a checklist. "Owner handles getting in" is as far as it goes.
- Notes for next visit are observations. Anything durable moves to clients.md only after the owner approves the exact line.
- Show the owner the checklist before the job. It's their standard the crew is carrying.
