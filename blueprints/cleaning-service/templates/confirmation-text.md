# Confirmation text

A short text confirming tomorrow's visit. Claude drafts it and shows the owner. Only the owner sends it.

## The skeleton

Hi [first name]! Just confirming we're set for tomorrow, [day], arriving around [arrival window from tomorrow's list]. [One personal touch if there is one — like "we'll give the kitchen some extra attention like you asked."] If anything's changed on your end, just reply here. — [owner or business name]

## How to fill it in

- Two or three sentences, no more. It's a text, not a letter.
- The arrival window comes from tomorrow's list. If nobody gave one, ask the owner — never guess a time.
- Never put a door code, alarm code, key location, or entry instruction in the text. If getting in needs arranging, "reply here and we'll sort out getting in" is the whole line, and the owner takes it from there.
- Only draft for clients who gave their number for texts. Not sure they did? Ask the owner first.
- Show the owner the exact text before it's sent. They send it — a past yes does not cover this one.
