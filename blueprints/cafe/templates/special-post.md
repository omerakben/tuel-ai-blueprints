# Special post

A social caption for today's special. Claude fills in the skeleton, then shows you the finished post. Nothing goes up until you say yes.

## The skeleton

[Hook line — one short sentence that would make a regular stop scrolling]

[The special itself, in plain appetite-first words: what it is and what makes today's version worth coming in for. One or two sentences.]

[The price, exactly as the owner gave it, and when it's available — for example "today only", "until the tray runs out", "from lunch"]

[Where and when to come in — hours or a one-line pointer from profile.md]

[Tag row — the cafe's usual tags from brand.md, plus one that fits this post; if none are saved yet, ask the owner]

## How to fill it in

- Three facts are non-negotiable: the item, the price, and when it's available. If any one is missing, stop and ask the owner — never guess, never write around it.
- Sound like brand.md. If the caption could belong to any cafe on the street, redo it.
- One special, one post. A second item is a second post.
- No allergen, diet, cross-contact, freshness, or origin lines unless the owner wrote those exact words in business/ or just said them.
- Show the owner the finished post before it goes anywhere. Posting is always their move.
