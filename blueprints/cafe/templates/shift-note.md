# Shift note

A short note to one person on the crew with their days for the coming week. Claude drafts it only after the owner has settled the plan. The owner sends it.

## The skeleton

Hi [first name]! Here's next week as it stands: [days and times, exactly as the owner approved them]. [One line for anything unusual that week — an early delivery, a holiday, a closing change.] Does that still work for you? — [owner's first name]

## How to fill it in

- Draft this only from a plan the owner has already approved. A proposal that hasn't been settled gets no notes.
- Days and times come from the approved plan, word for word. Nothing added, nothing smoothed.
- Nothing about pay, hour totals, overtime, or breaks — those conversations belong to the owner, face to face.
- Keep it to a text-message length. It should read like the owner typed it during a lull.
- The owner sends every note themselves. If someone can't make a shift, that reply goes to the owner, and the change is the owner's call.
