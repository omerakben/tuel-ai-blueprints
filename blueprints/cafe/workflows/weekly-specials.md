# Weekly specials hour

One hour a week to pick the week's specials and get their posts drafted — Claude does the writing, you make every call.

## When

Once a week, at a time you pick. Many owners use the quiet stretch after the morning rush on their slowest day. Say "let's plan the specials" and Claude starts.

## Steps

1. Look back. Claude reads last week's specials plan in reports/ and the week's day-close notes, and asks what sold out and what sat. If there is no record yet, Claude says so and starts fresh.
2. Gather the candidates. You name what could be a special this week: what's in surplus, what's in season, what the kitchen wants to try. One at a time. Claude never invents an item — if ideas run dry, it asks what's going on this week instead of making something up.
3. Set the facts. For each special you pick: the item, the price, and which day or days it runs. All three come from you. A special missing any one of them stays on the maybe list.
4. Draft the posts. One caption per special from templates/special-post.md, in your voice from business/brand.md. No allergen, diet, cross-contact, freshness, or origin lines unless your files or your words carry them.
5. Approve the batch. Claude lays every caption out in one place. You go item by item: yes, no, or change it. Changed items come back for another look. An item you skip is a no.
6. Make the posting plan. Claude ends with a short plan: each approved special, the day its post goes up, and where. You do the posting, on the day. The plan and the approved drafts are saved to reports/.

## What you get

- One caption per approved special, ready to copy on its day.
- A one-page week plan saved as reports/weekly-specials-YYYY-MM-DD.md, with the approved drafts underneath it.

## Never

- Claude never posts or sends anything. Not one word leaves this folder without you.
- Claude never invents an item, a price, a day, or a discount. Missing facts hold a special back until you fill them in.
- A yes covers that exact caption, that week. Last week's yes approves nothing this week, and staying quiet on an item is a no.
- No claims about allergens, diets, cross-contact, freshness, or origin beyond your own written or spoken words.
- Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
