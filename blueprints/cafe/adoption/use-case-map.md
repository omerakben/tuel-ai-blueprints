# Your AI adoption map

This map helps a cafe add Claude work at a pace the owner can trust. Maturity belongs to each routine, not to the whole cafe. You may stay at any stage with any routine for as long as you want.

No stage authorizes Claude to send, post, publish a shift, order stock, pay, promise availability, delete, or change an outside system. Use Manual mode for every new or changed routine until you review it again and approve its exact sources, output, and limits.

Only the owner may advance, pause, move back, resume, or retire a routine.

## Step 0. Readiness

Name the owner, choose one repeated job, and identify the current special, staff availability, stock count, or closing note Claude may read. Define the local draft you want and what should stop the work. Start with one special post or restock list. Stop if the offer, price, availability, staffing facts, food-safety boundary, or responsible owner are unclear.

## 1. Assisted

Work beside Claude on one special post using `skills/draft-the-special-post/`, or one restock list using `skills/prepare-a-restock-list/`.

- Provide confirmed product details or a current count.
- Check every item, price, time, quantity, and promise.
- Review the draft and receipt before you post, order, or change the day yourself.

Move only this routine forward when you have seen enough accurate work to trust its pattern.

## 2. Repeatable

Run a small manual batch of special posts, review replies, restock lists, and shift-plan drafts.

- Keep each public draft and planning item separate.
- Let a missing count, availability, or staff answer block only that item.
- Review the final drafts and owner decisions together.

## 3. Supervised operations

Turn one proven reports-only routine into a schedule. Start with the morning brief, end-of-day close, or quiet-morning promo.

Confirm the sources, run time, expected report, and pause conditions first. Every post, shift decision, order, payment, and customer promise remains your action.

## 4. Intent-led Business OS

Ask Claude to show which stock, staffing, special, or draft needs your attention. Review one exception brief with the evidence and next decision.

Claude may surface low stock, incomplete closing notes, staffing gaps, or drafts waiting for review. It never decides food safety, assigns a shift, orders stock, posts, or contacts anyone.

## Safe next uses

- Draft review replies from specific reviews.
- Prepare a shift-plan proposal from confirmed availability.
- Summarize weekly specials and flag missing inputs.

## Stop or defer

- Pause if prices, availability, counts, schedules, or policies are stale or disputed.
- Defer food-safety decisions, staffing decisions, ordering, payments, refunds, public posting, customer promises, access changes, and deletion.
- Return any routine to an earlier stage whenever you want more hands-on review.
