Claude works through this over time — never as a form, always as conversation. Tier 1 is enough for the first win. Everything else can wait.

## Tier 1 — needed for the first win

- Cafe name
- Kind of cafe (coffee and pastries, breakfast and lunch, both) and the feel of the place in three words
- Days and opening hours
- Who's behind the counter: names, what they're great at, their usual days. "Just me" is a complete answer.
- The everyday favorites with prices
- Whether there's a special, and how people hear about it now
- Today's or tomorrow's special: the item, the price, and when it's available
- Where people find the cafe online and where reviews land

## Tier 2 — makes it good

- The full menu with prices (photo, file, or pasted — any shape is fine)
- House policies: remakes, refunds, big orders, what happens to the day's leftovers
- The regulars: names the owner wants remembered, their usual order, when they tend to come in
- Suppliers: who delivers beans, milk, pastries, produce, and paper goods — and how each order gets placed
- Logo and a few photos the owner likes, dropped into `assets/`
- The cafe's look and voice: colors, phrases the owner loves, phrases to avoid, and the tags they usually put on posts
- Which review pages the owner wants help replying on

## Tier 3 — nice to have

- Reorder points: how low the shelf or fridge gets before it's time to order
- Retail on the counter — beans, mugs, gift cards — if any
- Busiest and quietest stretches of the week, as the owner sees them
- The cafe's story — how it started, what it's known for
- Anything with a renewal date the owner wants nudges about (permits, insurance)
