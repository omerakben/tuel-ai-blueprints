# The special post

Today's special, written up as a caption you can post anywhere — in your cafe's voice, ready in a couple of minutes.

## When to run this

- Each morning, once you've decided the special.
- Any time a new item deserves a shout — a bake that came out beautifully, a returning favorite.
- Before a slow stretch, when one good post might fill a few seats.

## What Claude reads first

- business/brand.md — how the cafe sounds and the tags you usually use.
- business/services.md — the everyday menu, so the special reads right next to it.
- business/profile.md — your hours and how people find you.
- templates/special-post.md — the shape every special post starts from.

You say the special out loud or paste it: the item, its price, and when it's available — all day, until it sells out, from lunch.

## Steps

1. Check the three facts: the item, the price, and when it's available. If any one of them is missing, Claude stops and asks you — this post never guesses.
2. Draft one caption from templates/special-post.md, in the voice from business/brand.md. One special, one post — a second item is a second post.
3. Keep the words inside your lane. Nothing about allergens, diets, what shares a counter or a fryer, how fresh something is, or where it comes from, unless those exact words are in your business/ files or you just said them.
4. Offer a photo if assets/ has one that fits. Claude suggests, you choose.
5. Show you the draft and wait. You edit, or say yes. A yes covers this exact caption, today.
6. Save it as reports/special-post-YYYY-MM-DD.md. You copy it into Instagram, Facebook, or wherever you post — posting is always yours.

## What you get

One file in reports/, a fresh one each run, named like reports/special-post-YYYY-MM-DD.md. Inside:

- The finished caption, ready to copy.
- The photo suggestion, if there was one.
- A line noting the price and hours you gave, so you can double-check at a glance.

## Never

- Never invent an item, a price, or a time it's available. Missing means stop and ask.
- Never claim anything is allergen-free or right for a diet, and never say what does or doesn't touch what in the kitchen. Only your written words, repeated exactly.
- Never add "fresh today", "baked this morning", or "locally sourced" unless you wrote it down or just said it.
- Never post anywhere, and never set a post up to go out later. The caption waits in reports/ for you.
- Never reuse yesterday's yes. Each post gets its own.

Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
