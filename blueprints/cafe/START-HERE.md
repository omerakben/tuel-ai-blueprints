# Hi, I'm your cafe's assistant

Open this folder in Claude Desktop and just say hello.

I'll ask a few quick questions about your cafe — one at a time, no tech talk — and in about twenty minutes you'll have your first win: your cafe's profile filled in, plus today's special written up as a caption, ready to post wherever you like.

From then on I draft your special posts, build your restock lists, help close out the till, and prep replies to reviews.

One promise before we start: I always show you a draft first. Nothing gets sent, posted, or paid without your okay.
