# Draft today's blooms post

Turns what's fresh in the cooler — in your own words — into a caption ready to post.

## When to run this

- After the market run, when the buckets are full and something deserves attention.
- Any day a flower is at its peak and you want people to know before it's gone.
- A slow afternoon, when a post might bring someone through the door.

## What Claude reads first

- business/brand.md — your voice, your usual tags, phrases you love and phrases you avoid.
- business/profile.md — how customers actually order, so the post ends the right way.
- templates/bloom-post.md — the shape the caption starts from.
- assets/ — only if you point at a photo to go with the post.

## Steps

1. Tell Claude what's in the cooler today, plain words: the stems, the colors, what just came in. A quick voice-note style ramble is perfect.
2. Claude reads the list back to you. Anything unclear becomes a question, not a guess — if you said "those big peachy ones", Claude asks what to call them.
3. Claude drafts one caption from templates/bloom-post.md, in the voice from brand.md. Only flowers you named go in. A price appears only if you gave one for this post. It ends with how to order, from profile.md.
4. If you want a photo with it, point at one in assets/ and Claude notes which photo goes with the caption in the report. You attach it when you post.
5. Claude shows you the draft and stops. You post it when you're happy — or say what to change and Claude redrafts.
6. Claude saves the caption to reports/ so you can reuse the good ones later.

## What you get

One file in reports/, a fresh one each run, named reports/blooms-post-YYYY-MM-DD.md:

- The caption, ready to copy and paste.
- The photo it pairs with, if you picked one.
- Any question that came up, like a stem that needs its real name.

## Never

- Never invent what's in the cooler. If you didn't name it today, it doesn't go in the post.
- Never post, and never set a post up to go out later. You paste, you post.
- Never make claims about pet safety, allergies, whether anything is edible, or how long flowers last — unless you supplied the exact wording, saved in policies.md or said just now.
- Never mention a price you didn't give for this post, and never write around it with "great prices".
- Never reuse an old caption as if it were today's — every post starts from what's actually in the buckets.

Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
