# Your AI adoption map

This map helps a florist add Claude work at a pace the owner can trust. Maturity belongs to each routine, not to the whole shop. You may stay at any stage with any routine for as long as you want.

No stage authorizes Claude to send, post, accept an order, promise availability, order stems, pay, delete, or change an outside system. Use Manual mode for every new or changed routine until you review it again and approve its exact sources, output, and limits.

Only the owner may advance, pause, move back, resume, or retire a routine.

## Step 0. Readiness

Name the owner, choose one repeated job, and identify the current order request, event brief, product list, stem count, or closing note Claude may read. Define the local draft you want and what should stop the work. Start with one custom-order reply or today's blooms post. Stop if date, flower, quantity, price, availability, delivery term, or responsible owner are unclear.

## 1. Assisted

Work beside Claude on one custom-order reply using `skills/draft-a-custom-order-reply/`, or one blooms post using `skills/draft-todays-blooms-post/`.

- Provide the real request or confirmed product details.
- Check every flower, quantity, date, price, availability, and promise.
- Review the draft and receipt before you reply or post yourself.

Move only this routine forward when you have seen enough accurate work to trust its pattern.

## 2. Repeatable

Run a small manual batch of order replies, event proposals, blooms posts, stem-order drafts, or closing notes.

- Keep each order and event separate.
- Let a missing count, date, or availability answer block only that item.
- Review the final drafts and owner decisions together.

## 3. Supervised operations

Turn one proven reports-only routine into a schedule. Start with the morning brief, end-of-day close, or upcoming-occasion preorder review.

Confirm the sources, run time, expected report, and pause conditions first. Every order acceptance, delivery promise, post, purchase, payment, and refund remains your action.

## 4. Intent-led Business OS

Ask Claude to show which orders, events, stems, or drafts need your attention. Review one exception brief with the evidence and next decision.

Claude may surface missing order details, low stems, unconfirmed dates, or drafts waiting for review. It never accepts an order, promises a delivery, orders stock, posts, or contacts anyone.

## Safe next uses

- Draft event proposals from confirmed details.
- Prepare a stem-order list from a current count.
- Summarize weekly orders and flag missing inputs.

## Stop or defer

- Pause if orders, event details, counts, prices, or availability are stale or disputed.
- Defer customer commitments, delivery promises, ordering, payments, refunds, public posting, access changes, and deletion.
- Return any routine to an earlier stage whenever you want more hands-on review.
