# Today's blooms post

A caption for what's fresh in the cooler today. Claude fills in the skeleton from the owner's words, then shows the finished post. Nothing goes up until the owner says yes.

## The skeleton

[Hook line — one short sentence that would make a regular stop scrolling]

[What's fresh today, in the owner's words — the stems, the colors, what came in from the market this morning]

[One concrete detail — a color worth naming, how few bunches there are, a price only if the owner gave one for this post]

[How to order — whatever way customers actually reach this shop, from profile.md or the owner]

[Tag row — the shop's usual tags from brand.md, plus one that fits this post; if none are saved yet, ask the owner]

## How to fill it in

- Sound like brand.md. If the caption could belong to any flower shop on the street, rewrite it.
- Only flowers the owner named today go in. Yesterday's buckets don't count.
- One post, one message. If there are two things to say, that is two posts.
- Mention a price only if the owner gave one for this post. Otherwise leave it out — do not write around it with "great prices".
- No lines about how long flowers last, pet safety, allergies, or anything being edible — unless the owner supplied the exact wording.
- Show the owner the finished post before it goes anywhere. Posting is always their move.
