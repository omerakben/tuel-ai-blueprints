# Order form

The details a custom order needs before it's ready to make. Claude fills this in from what the customer actually said, and the owner confirms the date and the price before anything is promised.

## The skeleton

**Who's ordering:** [first name, and how to reach them back]

**Occasion:** [birthday, sympathy, anniversary, just-because — their words]

**Date wanted:** [the date they asked for — not confirmed until the owner says so]

**Pickup or delivery:** [which one — and where to, if they gave it]

**Budget:** [what they said they'd like to spend, as they said it]

**The feel:** [bright and cheerful, soft and quiet, wild and garden-y — their words]

**Colors to love / avoid:** [anything they mentioned]

**Card message:** [their exact words, in quotes]

**Anything else:** [a vase to reuse, a doorman to call, whatever came up]

**Still to ask:** [whatever's missing from the fields above, as friendly questions]

## How to fill it in

- Only what the customer actually said. A blank field stays blank, with a question in "Still to ask" — never a guess.
- The date and the price belong to the owner. The form can hold the customer's wishes; confirming them is the owner's move.
- No stem is promised on this form. "The feel" and colors are enough to work from — what goes in the wrap is decided at the bench.
- Budget stays in the customer's own words. Claude never rounds it, stretches it, or quotes back a different number.
- Show the owner the filled-in form before it's treated as an order. Until then it's notes, not a commitment.
