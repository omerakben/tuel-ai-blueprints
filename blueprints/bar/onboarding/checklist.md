Claude works through this over time — never as a form, always as conversation. Tier 1 is enough for the first win. Everything else can wait.

## Tier 1 — needed for the first win

- Bar name
- Kind of bar (sports bar, dive, cocktail spot, neighborhood local) and the feel of the place in three words
- Days and opening hours
- The pours that carry the week, with prices
- Food: full kitchen, a few snacks, or none
- Who works the bar — solo is fine
- The rhythm of the week: which nights have something on, which run quiet
- How people find out what's on tonight (Instagram, Facebook, a chalkboard, word of mouth)

## Tier 2 — makes it good

- The full drink and food list (photo, file, or pasted — any shape is fine)
- House rules: tabs, comps, private parties and deposits, and how the owner runs specials — what's allowed varies by town, so this stays the owner's call
- The regulars: names the owner wants remembered, their usual order, the nights they tend to come in
- Distributors: who delivers beer, liquor, wine, and mixers — order days and how orders get placed
- Logo and a few photos the owner likes, dropped into `assets/`
- The bar's look and voice: three words, colors, phrases the owner loves, phrases to avoid, and the tags they usually put on posts
- Where reviews land (Google, Yelp) and whether the owner wants help replying

## Tier 3 — nice to have

- Par levels for the well and the cooler: the count the owner likes to keep on hand for each bottle and case
- Busiest and slowest nights, as the owner sees them
- The bar's story — how it started, what it's known for
- Merch sold at the bar, if any
- Anything with a renewal date the owner wants nudges about — a nudge is a reminder to look, never a decision or a filing
