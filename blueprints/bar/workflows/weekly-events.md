# Weekly events hour

One hour a week to plan the nights ahead — what's on, what to post, and when. Claude drafts everything, and you decide what goes out.

## When

Once a week, at a time you pick. Many owners use a quiet weekday afternoon before doors. Say "let's plan the week" and Claude starts.

## Steps

1. Look back. Claude reads last week's events plan in reports/ and checks what got posted and what brought people in, going by what you say. If there is no record yet, Claude says so and starts fresh.
2. Map the week. Claude asks what's on: games worth showing, live music, trivia or karaoke nights, a special you want to run, a holiday coming up. Real happenings only — Claude never invents an event, and never treats a special as running until you say it is.
3. Pin the facts. For each happening, Claude checks the two or three facts a post needs: what it is, when it starts, and the price if there's a special. Anything missing gets asked now, so the drafts write clean.
4. Draft the posts. Claude writes one post per happening using templates/event-post.md, in your voice from business/brand.md. Prices come from business/services.md or from you, never from memory or guesswork.
5. Spot the quiet nights. If a night has nothing on, Claude says so and offers one idea grounded in what's true — a menu item worth showing off, a regular's favorite pour, the patio in good weather. You take it or leave it; a pass means no draft.
6. Approve the batch. Claude lays everything out in one place — every post, together. You go item by item: say yes or no, or tell Claude what to change. Changed items come back for another look. An item you skip is a no. Only items with your fresh yes make the plan.
7. Make the posting plan. Claude ends with a short plan: each approved post, the day and rough time you will put it up, and where it goes. You do the posting. The plan and the approved drafts are saved to reports/weekly-events-[date].md.

## What you get

- One post per happening, approved by you and ready to copy.
- A one-page posting plan saved as reports/weekly-events-[date].md, with the approved drafts underneath it.

## Never

- Claude never posts or sends anything. Not one word leaves this folder without you.
- Claude never invents an event, a band, a price, or a packed house. If it is not in your business/ files, your assets/ folder, or something you just said, Claude asks.
- A yes covers that exact item, that week. Last week's yes approves nothing this week, and staying quiet on an item is a no.
- Nothing in the plan is aimed at minors or encourages heavy or fast drinking — no drinking-game language, no all-you-can-drink framing. Specials are never written as legal or final; you decide what runs.
- Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
