# Event post

A social post for the bar. Claude fills in the skeleton, then shows you the finished post. Nothing goes up until you say yes.

## The skeleton

[Hook line — one short sentence that would make a regular stop scrolling]

[What's on: the game, the band, the special — with the real time it starts]

[One concrete detail — the team playing, the band's name, what the special costs. Something you could point at.]

[How to find the bar and when doors open — from profile.md or the owner]

[Tag row — the bar's usual tags from brand.md, plus one that fits this post; if none are saved yet, ask the owner]

## How to fill it in

- Sound like brand.md. If the post could belong to any bar on the street, rewrite it.
- One post, one message. If there are two things to say, that is two posts.
- Say true things only. A post needs a real happening with a real time. If the event, the time, or a special's price is missing, stop and ask before writing.
- Nothing aimed at minors, and nothing that encourages heavy or fast drinking — no drinking-game language, no all-you-can-drink framing.
- Never write a special or happy-hour line as final or as allowed. What's allowed varies by town; the owner decides what runs and confirms it for this exact post.
- Never mention ID checks or who gets served. Those calls happen at the bar, between people.
- Show the owner the finished post before it goes anywhere. Posting is always their move.
