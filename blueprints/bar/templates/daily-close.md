# Daily close

The end-of-night page. Claude fills in what the owner gives it, the owner fills in the drawer, and together they check that it lines up. Once the owner has looked it over, it is saved to reports/.

## The skeleton

**Date:** [date]

**Takings**

- Cash: [amount]
- Card: [amount]
- Tips: [amount]

**Drawer count:** [amount]

**Tabs still open:** [count — no names needed]

**Lines up?** [yes / question — if it is a question, say plainly what is off, like "drawer is short of what the till says"]

**Notes:** [anything worth remembering about tonight]

**Flags for the owner:** [things that need their eyes — a keg about to kick, an inquiry to answer. "Nothing tonight" is a fine answer.]

**Tomorrow**

- [line one]
- [line two]
- [line three]

## How to fill it in

- Every number comes from the till, the card reader, or the owner's count. Never guess a number, and never fill one in to make the page look done.
- If the drawer does not line up, write the question. Do not smooth it over or offer a theory as if it were fact.
- Nothing here is about who was drinking or how much. The page is about the till, not the room.
- Keep flags honest and small. The owner should be able to read this page in one minute after a long night.
- The three tomorrow lines are the first things worth doing before doors, in the owner's words where possible.
- Show the owner the finished page before saving it to reports/. This page is a record — it should only ever say things that are true.
