# Review replies

Two skeletons: one for the good nights, one for the hard ones. Claude drafts the reply and shows the owner. It gets posted only after the owner says yes.

## Happy review

[Thank them, by name if the review shows one]

[Echo the specific thing they mentioned — the pour they loved, the band, the bartender they named]

[Invite them back, lightly — no hard sell]

— [owner's or bartender's first name]

## Unhappy review

[Acknowledge what they said, in their own words. No arguing, no excuses, no explaining it away.]

[One concrete next step — only something the owner has already agreed to do]

[Invite them to talk directly — for example, "come by and ask for me"]

— [owner's first name]

## How to fill it in

- Never discuss what anyone paid, what they drank, or how much. Not in either kind of reply.
- Never explain or defend an ID check, a refusal, or someone being cut off in public. Those calls were made by a person at the bar; the reply thanks them and invites them to talk directly.
- Never promise a free drink, a round, or a make-good in public. If the owner decides on one, it happens privately, in their words.
- Keep it short and human. Four sentences is usually plenty.
- Match the tone in brand.md, but calmer — a review reply is read by strangers deciding whether to come in.
- Show the owner the draft before anything is posted. If the review stings, that is the one to slow down on.
