# Private event reply

A warm reply to someone asking about a party or private event. Claude drafts it to gather the details; the owner decides everything and sends it themselves.

## The skeleton

Hi [first name] — thanks for thinking of [bar name] for your [occasion]!

[One warm line about the room or the night, true to profile.md — for example, "the back room fits a crowd nicely".]

So I can put real options together, a few quick things: [only the ones the inquiry didn't already answer]
- What date did you have in mind?
- Roughly how many people?
- Is there a rough budget for food and drinks?

[Closing line that promises a follow-up, not a booking — for example, "Once I have those I'll come back with what we can do."]

— [owner's first name]

## How to fill it in

- Ask only for what's missing. If they already said the date, don't ask again.
- Never say the date is free, and never say yes to the event. Availability is the owner's, checked fresh.
- Never quote a price, deposit, minimum, or package. If they asked about cost, say the owner will follow up once the date and headcount are set. If policies.md lists a deposit, you may say one exists — never the amount.
- Warm, short, and in the bar's voice from brand.md. This is a note from a person, not a booking system.
- Show the owner the draft before it goes anywhere. They send it from their own account.
