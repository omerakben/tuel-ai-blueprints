# Your AI adoption map

This map helps a bar add Claude work at a pace the owner can trust. Maturity belongs to each routine, not to the whole bar. You may stay at any stage with any routine for as long as you want.

No stage authorizes Claude to send, post, accept an event, order stock, pay, promise availability, delete, or change an outside system. Use Manual mode for every new or changed routine until you review it again and approve its exact sources, output, and limits.

Only the owner may advance, pause, move back, resume, or retire a routine.

## Step 0. Readiness

Name the owner, choose one repeated job, and identify the current event inquiry, confirmed offer, stock count, or closing note Claude may read. Define the local draft you want and what should stop the work. Start with one private-event reply or tonight's promo draft. Stop if price, capacity, date, alcohol-service policy, or ownership is unclear.

## 1. Assisted

Work beside Claude on one private-event reply using `skills/draft-a-private-event-reply/`, or one promo using `skills/draft-tonights-promo/`.

- Provide the real inquiry or confirmed event details.
- Check every date, price, capacity, offer, and promise.
- Review the draft and receipt before you reply or post yourself.

Move only this routine forward when you have seen enough accurate work to trust its pattern.

## 2. Repeatable

Run a small manual batch of event replies, review replies, promo drafts, or restock lists.

- Keep each event or public draft separate.
- Let a missing detail or policy question block only that item.
- Review the final drafts and owner decisions together.

## 3. Supervised operations

Turn one proven reports-only routine into a schedule. Start with the morning brief, end-of-day close, or slow-night promo.

Confirm the sources, run time, expected report, and pause conditions first. Every event acceptance, post, order, payment, and customer promise remains your action.

## 4. Intent-led Business OS

Ask Claude to show which events, stock items, or drafts need your attention. Review one exception brief with the evidence and next decision.

Claude may surface missing event details, low stock, unreviewed public drafts, or incomplete closing notes. It never decides service safety, accepts an event, orders stock, or contacts anyone.

## Safe next uses

- Draft review replies from specific reviews.
- Prepare a restock list from a current count.
- Summarize weekly events and flag missing details.

## Stop or defer

- Pause if event terms, stock counts, offers, or policies are stale or disputed.
- Defer alcohol-service and safety decisions, event commitments, ordering, payments, refunds, public posting, access changes, and deletion.
- Return any routine to an earlier stage whenever you want more hands-on review.
