# Draft a new-arrivals post

Turns a piece that just landed — described in chat or photographed into assets/ — into a ready-to-post caption. If the price or the sizes are missing, Claude stops and asks.

## When to run this

- A box arrived and something in it deserves the top of your feed.
- You photographed a new piece and dropped the shot in assets/.
- A restock landed and regulars have been asking when it's back.

## What Claude reads first

- business/brand.md — how the shop talks, so the caption sounds like you.
- business/services.md — your lines and price points, so names come out right.
- business/profile.md — how customers reach you, for the "come get it" line.
- assets/ — the photo, tag shot, or vendor sheet for this piece, if you added one.
- templates/arrival-post.md — the shape every caption starts from.

From you: what the piece is, what it costs, and which sizes came in. A sentence in chat or a photo in assets/ both work as a starting point.

## Steps

1. Tell Claude about the piece — describe it, or point at a photo in assets/.
2. Claude repeats back what it heard or saw: the piece, the line it belongs to, what stood out. Anything unclear, Claude asks before writing a word.
3. The fact check. Claude confirms the price and the sizes that came in. If either is missing from your files, the tag, or what you just said, Claude stops and asks — no caption gets drafted around a missing price.
4. The photo check. If the photo shows a customer, Claude asks whether that customer said yes to being posted. No confirmation, no photo — the caption can stand alone.
5. Claude drafts one caption using templates/arrival-post.md, in the voice from business/brand.md. Every claim about the piece — the fabric, the maker, the fit — comes from the tag, the vendor sheet, or your own words.
6. Claude shows you the draft and waits. Ask for a shorter one, a warmer one, a different hook — as many rounds as you like. Nothing moves until you say it's good.
7. You post it yourself, wherever you post. Claude saves the approved caption to reports/ so you can copy it whenever you're ready.
8. If the piece belongs on your line list, Claude shows you the exact line it would add to business/services.md and writes it only after your yes.

## What you get

One file saved as reports/arrival-post-YYYY-MM-DD.md: the caption you approved, the facts it used (price, sizes), and which photo in assets/ goes with it, if any.

## Never

- Never draft around a missing price or missing sizes — stop and ask, every time.
- Never write "last one!", "selling fast", or any scarcity line unless the owner confirmed the count is real.
- Never invent a sale, a discount, or a price drop to make the post land.
- Never claim what a piece is made of, where it comes from, or how it fits unless it comes from the tag, the vendor sheet, or the owner.
- Never use a photo with a customer in it until the owner confirms that customer said yes.
- Never post anything — the caption waits in reports/ until the owner posts it themselves.

Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
