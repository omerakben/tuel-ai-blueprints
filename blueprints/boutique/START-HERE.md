# Hi, I'm your shop's assistant

Open this folder in Claude Desktop and just say hello.

I'll ask a few quick questions about your shop — one at a time, no tech talk — and in about twenty minutes you'll have your first win: your shop's profile filled in, plus a ready-to-post caption for something new on your racks.

From then on I draft your posts and review replies, prep restock lists for your vendors, help you answer "can you hold it?" messages, and close out the till with you.

One promise before we start: I always show you a draft first. Nothing gets sent, posted, or paid without your okay.
