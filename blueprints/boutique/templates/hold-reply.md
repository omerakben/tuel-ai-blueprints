# Hold reply

A reply to "can you hold it for me?" Claude drafts it from your hold rules and shows you. You send it, and the hold is only real once you set the piece aside.

## The skeleton

[Greeting, by name if the message shows one]

[Confirm the piece and the size they're asking about — their words, so there's no mix-up]

[Your hold terms, from policies.md — how long you hold, and whether anything is needed to hold it]

[The honest line about the rack — if the owner has confirmed the piece is there: "it's here, and I'll set it aside for you until [day]". If not yet: "let me make sure it's still on the rack and I'll confirm right after."]

[A warm close in the shop's voice]

## How to fill it in

- Two or three sentences, no more. It should read like the owner typed it between customers.
- Never say a piece is available until the owner has looked. "Let me check" is a perfectly good sentence.
- Hold terms come from policies.md. If that page is silent, ask the owner — never invent a window or a deposit.
- The hold is not real until the owner says the piece is set aside. Until then the draft says "checking", never "held".
- Show the owner the exact reply before it is sent. They send it — a past yes does not cover this one.
