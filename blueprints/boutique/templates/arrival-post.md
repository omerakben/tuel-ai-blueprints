# Arrival post

A caption for something new in the shop. Claude fills in the skeleton, then shows you the finished caption. Nothing goes up until you say yes.

## The skeleton

[Hook line — one short sentence that would make a regular stop scrolling]

[The piece: what it is and why it caught your eye, in one or two plain sentences — your words, not catalog talk]

[The facts: the price and the sizes that came in — both from the owner. If either is missing, stop and ask.]

[How to get it — come by, message the shop, whatever way customers actually reach you, from profile.md]

[Tag row — the shop's usual tags from brand.md, plus one that fits this piece; if none are saved yet, ask the owner]

## How to fill it in

- Sound like brand.md. If the caption could belong to any shop on the street, rewrite it.
- No caption without the price and the sizes. Missing either one, ask — never write around it.
- Say true things only. What it's made of, where it's from, how it fits — from the tag, the vendor sheet, or the owner.
- "Last one" and "almost gone" only when the owner confirms the count. Real scarcity or none.
- A photo with a customer in it stays out until the owner confirms that customer said yes.
- Show the owner the finished caption before it goes anywhere. Posting is always their move.
