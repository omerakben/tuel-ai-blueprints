# What to collect, in order

Claude works through this over time — never as a form, always as conversation. Tier 1 is enough for the first win. Everything else can wait.

## Tier 1 — needed for the first win

- Shop name
- What the shop sells, and the feel of the place in three words
- Days and opening hours
- Who works here — just the owner is a fine answer
- Best-selling lines or categories with rough price points
- How customers usually reach the shop (walk-ins, messages, calls, a website)
- One true new arrival, with its price and the sizes that came in — that's the first post

## Tier 2 — makes it good

- The fuller price or line list (photo, file, or pasted — any shape is fine)
- House policies: returns and exchanges, holds, special orders, markdowns
- The regulars: names the owner wants remembered, what they love, who they shop for
- Vendors: who supplies each line, how orders get placed, usual lead times
- Logo and a few photos the owner likes, dropped into `assets/`
- The shop's look and voice: three words, colors, phrases the owner loves, phrases to avoid, and the tags they usually put on posts
- Where reviews land (Google, Yelp) and whether the owner wants help replying

## Tier 3 — nice to have

- Reorder points: how low stock gets before it's time to order
- The markdown rhythm: when things usually get marked down — the owner's call, always
- Busiest and slowest days, as the owner sees them
- The shop's story — how it started, what it's known for
- Gift cards, loyalty punch cards, or store credit, if the shop does any of that
- Anything with a renewal date the owner wants nudges about (lease, insurance)
