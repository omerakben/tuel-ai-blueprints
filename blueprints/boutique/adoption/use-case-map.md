# Your AI adoption map

This map helps a boutique add Claude work at a pace the owner can trust. Maturity belongs to each routine, not to the whole shop. You may stay at any stage with any routine for as long as you want.

No stage authorizes Claude to send, post, place a hold, order stock, pay, promise availability, delete, or change an outside system. Use Manual mode for every new or changed routine until you review it again and approve its exact sources, output, and limits.

Only the owner may advance, pause, move back, resume, or retire a routine.

## Step 0. Readiness

Name the owner, choose one repeated job, and identify the current product details, customer request, stock count, or closing note Claude may read. Define the local draft you want and what should stop the work. Start with one hold-request reply or new-arrivals post. Stop if item, size, price, stock, hold policy, or responsible owner are unclear.

## 1. Assisted

Work beside Claude on one hold-request reply using `skills/draft-a-hold-request-reply/`, or one arrivals post using `skills/draft-a-new-arrivals-post/`.

- Provide the real request or confirmed product details.
- Check every item, size, price, availability, and hold term.
- Review the draft and receipt before you reply or post yourself.

Move only this routine forward when you have seen enough accurate work to trust its pattern.

## 2. Repeatable

Run a small manual batch of hold replies, arrivals posts, review replies, or restock lists.

- Keep each customer request and public draft separate.
- Let a missing product detail or policy question block only that item.
- Review the final drafts and owner decisions together.

## 3. Supervised operations

Turn one proven reports-only routine into a schedule. Start with the morning brief, end-of-day close, or slow-day promo.

Confirm the sources, run time, expected report, and pause conditions first. Every hold, post, order, payment, refund, and customer promise remains your action.

## 4. Intent-led Business OS

Ask Claude to show which stock items, holds, or drafts need your attention. Review one exception brief with the evidence and next decision.

Claude may surface low stock, expiring holds, missing product details, or public drafts waiting for review. It never places a hold, orders stock, posts, or contacts anyone.

## Safe next uses

- Draft review replies from specific reviews.
- Prepare a restock list from a current count.
- Summarize weekly merchandising and flag missing inputs.

## Stop or defer

- Pause if stock, prices, hold terms, or product details are stale or disputed.
- Defer customer commitments, ordering, payments, refunds, public posting, employment decisions, access changes, and deletion.
- Return any routine to an earlier stage whenever you want more hands-on review.
