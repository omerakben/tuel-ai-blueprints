# Operations

This owner-owned folder holds current work: open items, review queues, setup notes, and short-lived coordination state.

- Keep durable approved facts in `business/`.
- Keep incoming exports and temporary source material in `inbox/`.
- Keep finished drafts, briefs, review notes, and receipts in `reports/`.
- Do not store passwords, access tokens, private keys, complete payment-card or bank details, government identifiers, payroll detail, health records, or privileged legal material here.

The downloadable folder ships with this guide only. Update tools never overwrite an installed owner's operations files.
