# Your business's files

Drop your logo, your rate sheet, and a few photos of finished work in this folder.
Nothing is required — an empty folder is fine.
Claude will ask about these during your first chat and use whatever you've added.

## Do not add

Keep passwords, access codes, payment-card details, bank details, government IDs, health records, private legal material, and files you do not have permission to use out of this folder. Put changing daily lists and exports in `inbox/`, not here. Ask the owner when a file is unclear.
