# Connect your tools (all optional)

Your first win needs none of this. Claude works from this folder and what you tell it. Connect things later, and only if they save you time.

A connected tool may be able to change records as well as read them. Start in Manual mode, review what the tool can do, and keep this folder draft-only. You still perform every outside action.

## Gmail

When your Gmail is connected, estimate and reminder drafts land in your email ready to send. You still press send, every time.

## Square (or whatever takes your payments)

When the tool that takes your payments is connected, the month-end tidy-up can read the month's payments instead of you reading the numbers out. You review the summary before it goes anywhere.

## Google Business Profile

If your business listing can be connected, Claude helps you keep your hours and service area current and prepares replies to reviews, ready for you to paste. Posting is always yours.

## Instagram and Facebook

No connection needed here at all. If you like showing off a finished job, Claude writes the post and the caption; you copy it over when you're happy with it. Posting is always yours.

---

A mention here does not switch anything on. If you want one of these, say so and Claude walks you through it. You can turn any of them off whenever you like.
