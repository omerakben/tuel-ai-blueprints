# Estimate follow-up note

A short, friendly nudge for an estimate that went quiet. Claude drafts it and shows the owner. Only the owner sends it.

## The skeleton

Hi [first name]! Just checking in on the estimate I sent over [when — "last week", "a couple of weeks back"] for [the job, in a few words]. [One easy question — "still thinking it over?" or "want me to pencil you in for [rough timeframe]?"] [An easy out — "and if now's not the time, no worries at all, just let me know."]

## How to fill it in

- Two or three sentences. It's a nudge, not a pitch.
- It should read like the owner typed it at a red light. If it sounds like a company wrote it, redo it.
- The job and the timing come from the saved estimate in reports/ — never from memory.
- No new numbers, no discounts, no "prices going up soon". If the owner wants to change the offer, that's a fresh estimate, not a nudge.
- Show the owner the exact note before it is sent. They send it — a past yes does not cover this one.
