# Job note

A short record of a day's work on a job, written at the end of the day while it's fresh. It lives in reports/ and makes month-end painless.

## The skeleton

**Job:** [customer and the job, in a few words]

**Where:** [area or street, as exact as the owner wants — never a door code, an alarm code, or where a key is kept]

**What got done:** [plain lines — done says done, half-done says half-done]

**Materials used:** [what came off the truck or out of the store run]

**Time spent:** [hours, from the owner]

**Invoiced?** [yes / not yet / paid — in the owner's words]

**Worth flagging:** [anything spotted for the owner to raise with the customer — a soft board, a slow drain. A note for the owner, never advice sent to anyone.]

## How to fill it in

- Everything comes from what the owner says. No filling in, no tidying the truth.
- "Worth flagging" is a nudge for the owner, never a diagnosis. If it touches see-it-first work (electrical, gas, structural, roofing, asbestos, lead, mold), the flag says "worth a proper look" and stops there.
- Never write down a door code, an alarm code, or where a key is hidden. If one comes up, say so and leave it out.
- Keep it short enough to write from the truck. Five minutes, tops.
