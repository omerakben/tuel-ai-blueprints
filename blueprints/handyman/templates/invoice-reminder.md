# Invoice reminder

A friendly reminder about finished work that hasn't been paid. Claude drafts it and shows the owner. Only the owner sends it.

## The skeleton

Hi [first name]! Hope [something small and true — the shelves, the new door] is working out well. Just a friendly reminder that the invoice for [the job] from [when] is still open — [amount, only if the owner gave it]. [How to pay, in the owner's usual words.] [Warm close — "thanks so much, and give me a shout if anything's off."]

## How to fill it in

- Friendly first, every time. Most unpaid invoices are forgetfulness, and the note should assume so.
- The amount and the date come from the owner's list — never from memory, never rounded.
- No threats and no legal talk. No late fee either, unless policies.md allows one and the owner said yes for this exact invoice.
- If it's the second or third reminder, the tone can firm up a notch — the owner decides how much.
- Show the owner the exact note before it is sent. They send it, every time.
