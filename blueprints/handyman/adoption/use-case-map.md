# Your AI adoption map

This map helps a handyman business add Claude work at a pace the owner can trust. Maturity belongs to each routine, not to the whole business. You may stay at any stage with any routine for as long as you want.

No stage authorizes Claude to send, book a job, promise a price or time, decide a repair is safe, order materials, pay, delete, or change an outside system. Use Manual mode for every new or changed routine until you review it again and approve its exact sources, output, and limits.

Only the owner may advance, pause, move back, resume, or retire a routine.

## Step 0. Readiness

Name the owner, choose one repeated job, and identify the current service request, site notes, approved rate, schedule, or materials note Claude may read. Define the local draft you want and what should stop the work. Start with one job estimate or materials list. Stop if scope, rate, site condition, safety issue, permit question, or responsible owner are unclear.

## 1. Assisted

Work beside Claude on one estimate using `skills/draft-a-job-estimate/`, or one materials list using `skills/prepare-a-materials-list/`.

- Provide the real request and current site notes.
- Check every task, quantity, amount, date, and assumption.
- Review the draft and receipt before you contact, book, promise, or buy anything yourself.

Move only this routine forward when you have seen enough accurate work to trust its pattern.

## 2. Repeatable

Run a small manual batch of estimates, estimate follow-ups, invoice reminders, week plans, or materials lists.

- Keep each customer and job separate.
- Let missing scope, site facts, or rates block only that item.
- Review the final drafts and owner decisions together.

## 3. Supervised operations

Turn one proven reports-only routine into a schedule. Start with the morning brief, end-of-day close, or estimate chase.

Confirm the sources, run time, expected report, and pause conditions first. Every booking, message, promise, material order, payment, and safety decision remains your action.

## 4. Intent-led Business OS

Ask Claude to show which jobs, estimates, materials, or reminders need your attention. Review one exception brief with the evidence and next decision.

Claude may surface stale estimates, missing job details, schedule conflicts, material gaps, or drafts waiting for review. It never decides a repair is safe, books work, orders materials, or contacts anyone.

## Safe next uses

- Draft estimate follow-ups from a current owner-confirmed list.
- Prepare a weekly plan from confirmed jobs.
- Draft invoice reminders from current records.

## Stop or defer

- Pause if scope, site notes, rates, schedule, or materials information are stale or disputed.
- Defer structural, electrical, plumbing, permit, and safety judgment, bookings, orders, payments, customer promises, access changes, and deletion.
- Return any routine to an earlier stage whenever you want more hands-on review.
