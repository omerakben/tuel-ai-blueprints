# What to collect, in order

<!-- TUEL:CONTROL-CHECKLIST:START -->
## Control choices

- Name the person who approves decisions and outside actions.
- Confirm the local time zone and usual working days.
- List which files and connected tools Claude may read for routine work.
- Choose how long temporary exports in `inbox/` should be kept. Claude may flag old files but never deletes them.
- Name the accountant, lawyer, technician, or other qualified person who reviews work outside Claude's lane, when relevant.
- Choose one repeated job for the first Assisted routine. Record how it works today, its approved sources, a useful result, and a stop signal.
- Review saved evidence before advancing a routine. A stage change never authorizes sending, posting, paying, filing, signing, deciding, or deleting.
<!-- TUEL:CONTROL-CHECKLIST:END -->

Claude works through this over time — never as a form, always as conversation. Tier 1 is enough for the first win. Everything else can wait.

## Tier 1 — needed for the first win

- Business name
- The kind of work taken on, and the work not touched
- The area covered
- Who does the work — solo is fine; helpers get a name, strengths, and days
- Working days and hours
- How the owner charges (hourly, flat rate, a mix), with rates for the common jobs
- How customers reach the business (calls, texts, an app, word of mouth)
- How estimates go out today, and how people pay

## Tier 2 — makes it good

- The full rate sheet (photo, file, or pasted — any shape is fine)
- House policies: deposits, payment terms, callbacks and redos, how long an estimate stays good
- Repeat customers: names the owner wants remembered, the jobs they call about, how they like to be reached
- The stores and suppliers: which store for what, and how the owner buys
- Logo and a few photos of finished work, dropped into `assets/`
- How the business sounds: a few words for the voice, phrases the owner uses, phrases to avoid
- Where reviews land (Google, Yelp) and whether the owner wants drafted replies

## Tier 3 — nice to have

- What stays stocked on the truck, so shopping lists skip it
- Busiest and slowest seasons, as the owner sees them
- The story of the business — how it started, what it's known for
- Anything with a renewal date the owner wants nudges about (insurance, van registration). Nudges only — Claude never tracks or claims what a license covers.
