# Your AI adoption map

This map helps a food truck add Claude work at a pace the owner can trust. Maturity belongs to each routine, not to the whole business. You may stay at any stage with any routine for as long as you want.

No stage authorizes Claude to send, post, accept an event, choose a location, order stock, pay, promise availability, delete, or change an outside system. Use Manual mode for every new or changed routine until you review it again and approve its exact sources, output, and limits.

Only the owner may advance, pause, move back, resume, or retire a routine.

## Step 0. Readiness

Name the owner, choose one repeated job, and identify the current route, event inquiry, menu, stock count, or closing note Claude may read. Define the local draft you want and what should stop the work. Start with one location post or prep list. Stop if location, date, menu, quantity, price, permit, food-safety boundary, or responsible owner are unclear.

## 1. Assisted

Work beside Claude on one location post using `skills/draft-todays-location-post/`, or one prep list using `skills/build-a-prep-list/`.

- Provide the confirmed route, menu, or event details.
- Check every location, time, item, quantity, price, and promise.
- Review the draft and receipt before you post, prep, or reply yourself.

Move only this routine forward when you have seen enough accurate work to trust its pattern.

## 2. Repeatable

Run a small manual batch of location posts, event replies, prep lists, restock lists, or closing notes.

- Keep each event and production day separate.
- Let a missing route, quantity, or availability answer block only that item.
- Review the final drafts and owner decisions together.

## 3. Supervised operations

Turn one proven reports-only routine into a schedule. Start with the morning brief, end-of-day close, or tomorrow's location post.

Confirm the sources, run time, expected report, and pause conditions first. Every route choice, event acceptance, post, purchase, payment, and customer promise remains your action.

## 4. Intent-led Business OS

Ask Claude to show which route details, events, prep items, stock, or drafts need your attention. Review one exception brief with the evidence and next decision.

Claude may surface missing location details, prep gaps, low stock, unconfirmed events, or drafts waiting for review. It never decides food safety, chooses a route, accepts an event, orders stock, posts, or contacts anyone.

## Safe next uses

- Draft event-inquiry replies from confirmed terms.
- Prepare restock lists from current counts.
- Summarize the weekly route and flag missing inputs.

## Stop or defer

- Pause if routes, menus, counts, event details, or policies are stale or disputed.
- Defer food-safety and permit decisions, route commitments, event acceptance, ordering, payments, refunds, public posting, access changes, and deletion.
- Return any routine to an earlier stage whenever you want more hands-on review.
