# Prep list

The one-pager for tomorrow's service. Claude fills it in from tomorrow's plan and the menu, the owner checks it, and it rides along to the kitchen and the truck.

## The skeleton

**For:** [day and date]

**Spot and hours:** [tomorrow's confirmed spot and hours — leave as a question if not confirmed yet]

**Expecting:** [the owner's read on the day — slow lunch, big event, festival crowd]

**Batch tonight**

- [item] — [amount] — [what it's for]
- [item] — [amount] — [what it's for]

**Buy in the morning**

- [item] — [amount] — [where you usually get it, from suppliers.md]

**Load the truck**

- [everything that has to make it on board — food, packaging, the cash box, propane]

**Questions for you**

- [anything Claude couldn't place — no par level, no count given, spot not confirmed]

## How to fill it in

- Every amount comes from the owner's counts, the par levels in suppliers.md, or the owner's read on the crowd. Never a guess.
- The buy list is a shopping list, not an order. If something also sits below its restock point, say so — that's a restock-list job.
- No food-safety calls. How long something keeps and what can be held overnight is the owner's rule; repeat it only if the owner wrote it down.
- Keep it one page. It gets read at dawn with one hand on a coffee.
- Show the owner the finished list before saving it to reports/.
