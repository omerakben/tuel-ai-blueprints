# Event inquiry reply

The reply that goes back to someone asking to book the truck. Claude drafts it and shows the owner. Only the owner sends it.

## The skeleton

Hi [first name],

[One warm line back — thanks for thinking of the truck, plus something specific from their message.]

[If they gave details, reflect them back so they know they were heard: the date, the kind of event, the crowd they mentioned.]

To see if we can make it work, could you tell me:

- What date, and what serving times do you have in mind?
- Where is it, and is there room for a truck our size to park and serve?
- Roughly how many people?
- Is there power on site, or should we plan to run on our own?

[What happens next — for example: "Once I have these, [owner's name] will come back to you about availability and what it would cost."]

[Sign-off in the truck's voice, with the truck's name.]

## How to fill it in

- Warm, short, and specific. It should read like the owner typed it during a lull at the window.
- Ask only for what's actually missing — if they already gave the date, don't ask again.
- No commitments. The reply never says yes, never holds a date, and never promises the truck can be there.
- Numbers only if the owner gave them for this reply, and always called an estimate — the final price is the owner's call after seeing the whole picture.
- Nothing about allergens, cross-contact, or sourcing beyond the exact words in services.md.
- Show the owner the exact reply before it is sent. They send it from their own account, every time.
