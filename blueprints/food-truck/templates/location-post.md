# Location post

The daily "here's where we are" post. Claude fills in the skeleton from a confirmed spot, then shows you the finished post. Nothing goes up until you say yes.

## The skeleton

[Hook line — one short sentence with today's day and the spot, the way a regular would say it]

[The hours, plain: when the window opens and when it closes]

[One menu highlight — an item from services.md, described only in words the owner has used]

[How to find the truck at this spot — a corner, a landmark, "look for the [color] truck"]

[Tag row — the truck's usual tags from brand.md, plus one for the spot or the day; if none are saved yet, ask the owner]

## How to fill it in

- Only a confirmed spot goes in a post — the owner has said permission, permit, and hours are squared away for this day. Not sure? Ask before writing.
- Sound like brand.md. If the post could belong to any truck in town, rewrite it.
- Hours come from the owner for this day, not from the usual pattern. A rainy-day early close matters more than a pretty caption.
- Say true things only about the food. No allergen, freshness, or sourcing claims beyond the exact words in services.md — "ask us at the window" is the fallback.
- Mention a price only if the owner gave one for this post. Otherwise leave it out.
- Show the owner the finished post before it goes anywhere. Posting is always their move.
