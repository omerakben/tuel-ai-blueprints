# Today's location post

Turns today's confirmed spot and hours into a ready-to-post caption with one menu highlight — you paste it and post it when you're happy.

## When to run this

Run it in the morning before you roll, or the night before if today's spot is already locked in. It's the daily heartbeat: people can't line up if they don't know where you are.

If the evening helper in schedules/tomorrows-location-post.md already drafted today's post, run this only to change it — no need to start over.

## What Claude reads first

- business/profile.md — your regular spots and where you announce the day's location.
- business/services.md — your menu, for the highlight line. What's written there is all Claude will say about any item.
- business/brand.md — how you talk, so the post sounds like you.
- templates/location-post.md — the shape the post starts from.

From you: today's spot, today's hours, and the menu highlight you want to lead with. Claude checks all three are confirmed before it writes a word.

## Steps

1. Confirm the spot. Claude asks: where are you setting up today, and what hours? Then one more: is this spot squared away — permission and permit sorted? Your word is enough. If the spot, the hours, or the permission side is unconfirmed, Claude stops and asks — it never posts a guess and never fills the gap with where you usually are.
2. Pick the highlight. Claude suggests one menu item from business/services.md, or you name one — a special, a comeback item, whatever you want people craving today. If the item isn't in services.md and you haven't described it, Claude asks rather than inventing a description.
3. Draft the post from templates/location-post.md, in the voice from business/brand.md: the spot, the hours, the highlight, how to find you, your usual tags.
4. Show you the draft and wait. You say yes, or say what to change and Claude redrafts. A yes confirms the words — the posting itself is yours, from your own account.
5. Save the approved post to reports/ so you can copy it from there any time today.

## What you get

One file in reports/, a fresh one each run, named like reports/location-post-YYYY-MM-DD.md. Inside:

- The approved post, ready to copy and paste.
- The spot and hours it announces, so tomorrow-you knows what today-you promised.
- Anything still open — for example, a spot you mentioned but haven't confirmed yet.

## Never

- Never announce a spot the owner hasn't confirmed for today — permission, permit, and hours, all three. Showing up somewhere the last three Fridays is a pattern, not a confirmation.
- Never invent hours, a special, or a price. If it isn't in business/ or something the owner just said, ask.
- Never make claims about allergens, cross-contact, freshness, or sourcing beyond the exact words in business/services.md. When in doubt, the post says "ask us at the window".
- Never post, never schedule a post, and never touch any account. The owner pastes and posts, every time.
- Never reuse yesterday's yes. A new day's post is a new draft and a fresh yes.

Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
