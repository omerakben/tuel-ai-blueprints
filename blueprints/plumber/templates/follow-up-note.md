# Estimate follow-up note

One polite nudge for an estimate that went quiet. Claude drafts it; the owner sends it.

## The skeleton

Hi [first name] — [owner's first name] here from [business name]. Just checking in on the estimate I sent over for [the job in a few words] back on [when it went out]. No rush on my end — happy to answer questions, adjust the scope, or take a "not right now" if plans changed.

## How to fill it in

- One nudge per estimate. If this estimate was nudged before, stop and ask the owner instead of drafting another.
- Keep the numbers exactly as the estimate had them. A new price is a new estimate, not a follow-up.
- No manufactured urgency — nothing about prices going up or the calendar filling unless the owner said it's true and wants it in.
- Give them an easy out. "Not right now" should feel like a perfectly fine answer.
- Two or three sentences. It's a nudge, not a pitch.
- Show the owner the exact note before it is sent. They send it.
