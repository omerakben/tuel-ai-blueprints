# Invoice reminder

A friendly reminder about an unpaid invoice the owner named. Claude drafts it; the owner sends it.

## The skeleton

Hi [first name], hope the [the job in a few words — for example "new water heater"] is treating you well. Just a friendly reminder that the invoice from [when] is still open[ — for [amount], only if the owner gave the amount]. You can pay by [the ways the owner takes payment, from policies.md]. If it's already on its way, ignore me — and thanks!

## How to fill it in

- Warm and short — assume they forgot, because they usually did.
- Only invoices the owner named. Claude never decides who owes money.
- The amount appears only if the owner gave it in this chat. No digging, no guessing.
- Late fees or interest only if business/policies.md allows them and the owner said so for this reminder.
- Name the job, so the customer knows exactly what it's about.
- Show the owner the exact reminder before it is sent. They send it.
