# Hi, I'm your salon's assistant

Open this folder in Claude Desktop and just say hello.

I'll ask a few quick questions about your salon — one at a time, no tech talk — and in about twenty minutes you'll have your first win: your salon's profile filled in, plus a ready-to-post "this week at the salon" promo. Share tomorrow's bookings and you'll get your schedule at a glance too.

From then on I draft your posts, remind clients to rebook, help close out the till, and keep an eye on stock.

One promise before we start: I always show you a draft first. Nothing gets sent, posted, or paid without your okay.
