# Your AI adoption map

This map helps a salon add Claude work at a pace the owner can trust. Maturity belongs to each routine, not to the whole salon. You may stay at any stage with any routine for as long as you want.

No stage authorizes Claude to send, post, book or cancel an appointment, promise a service or price, order stock, pay, refund, delete, or change an outside system. Use Manual mode for every new or changed routine until you review it again and approve its exact sources, output, and limits.

Only the owner may advance, pause, move back, resume, or retire a routine.

## Step 0. Readiness

Name the owner, choose one repeated job, and identify the current booking request, approved service list, appointment notes, stock count, or review Claude may read. Define the local draft you want and what should stop the work. Start with one booking reply or promo draft. Stop if service, stylist, date, price, availability, contact preference, health information, or responsible owner are unclear.

## 1. Assisted

Work beside Claude on one booking reply using `skills/book-appointment/`, or one promo using `skills/fill-the-chair/`.

- Provide the real request or confirmed opening.
- Check every service, stylist, date, price, availability, and promise.
- Review the draft and receipt before you reply, book, or post yourself.

Move only this routine forward when you have seen enough accurate work to trust its pattern.

## 2. Repeatable

Run a small manual batch of booking replies, rebooking reminders, chair-fill drafts, review replies, or stock lists.

- Keep each client and appointment separate.
- Let missing availability, consent, or service information block only that item.
- Review the final drafts and owner decisions together.

## 3. Supervised operations

Turn one proven reports-only routine into a schedule. Start with the morning brief, end-of-day close, or slow-day promo.

Confirm the sources, run time, expected report, and pause conditions first. Every booking, cancellation, message, post, order, payment, and refund remains your action.

## 4. Intent-led Business OS

Ask Claude to show which chair gaps, rebooking opportunities, stock items, or drafts need your attention. Review one exception brief with the evidence and next decision.

Claude may surface unfilled openings, due-back reminders, low stock, incomplete closes, or replies waiting for review. It never gives health guidance, books, cancels, orders stock, posts, or contacts anyone.

## Safe next uses

- Draft rebooking reminders from owner-approved client notes.
- Prepare reorder lists from current counts.
- Summarize weekly marketing and flag missing inputs.

## Stop or defer

- Pause if appointment facts, consent, prices, contact choices, stock counts, or service details are stale or disputed.
- Defer health and safety judgment, bookings, cancellations, ordering, payments, refunds, public posting, employment decisions, access changes, and deletion.
- Return any routine to an earlier stage whenever you want more hands-on review.
