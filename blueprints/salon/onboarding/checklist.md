# What to collect, in order

<!-- TUEL:CONTROL-CHECKLIST:START -->
## Control choices

- Name the person who approves decisions and outside actions.
- Confirm the local time zone and usual working days.
- List which files and connected tools Claude may read for routine work.
- Choose how long temporary exports in `inbox/` should be kept. Claude may flag old files but never deletes them.
- Name the accountant, lawyer, technician, or other qualified person who reviews work outside Claude's lane, when relevant.
<!-- TUEL:CONTROL-CHECKLIST:END -->

Claude works through this over time — never as a form, always as conversation. Tier 1 is enough for the first win. Everything else can wait.

## Tier 1 — needed for the first win

- Salon name
- Kind of salon (barbering, beauty, both) and the feel of the place in three words
- Days and opening hours
- Number of chairs
- Top services with price and rough duration
- Who works the chairs, what they're great at, which days
- How clients book today (calls, messages, a booking tool)

## Tier 2 — makes it good

- The full service and price list (photo, file, or pasted — any shape is fine)
- House policies: cancellations, deposits, late arrivals, redos
- The regulars: names the owner wants remembered, their usual service, anything they always ask for
- Suppliers: who delivers color, product, towels — and how orders get placed
- Logo and a few photos the owner likes, dropped into `assets/`
- The salon's look and voice: three words, colors, phrases the owner loves, phrases to avoid, and the tags they usually put on posts
- Where reviews land (Google, Yelp) and whether the owner wants help replying

## Tier 3 — nice to have

- Reorder points: how low stock gets before it's time to order
- Retail products sold at the front, if any
- Busiest and slowest days, as the owner sees them
- The salon's story — how it started, what it's known for
- How each stylist takes their pay (chair rent, commission), only if the owner wants the daily close to track it
- Anything with a renewal date the owner wants nudges about (licenses, insurance)
