# Weekly marketing hour

One hour a week to plan your posts and reminder texts — Claude drafts them, and you decide what goes out.

## When

Once a week, at a time you pick. Many owners use Monday morning coffee or the quiet stretch of a slow afternoon. Say "let's do the marketing hour" and Claude starts.

## Steps

1. Look back. Claude reads last week's marketing plan in reports/ and checks what went out and what people responded to. If there is no record yet, Claude says so and starts fresh.
2. Find something real to talk about. Claude looks for actual happenings: open slots in this week's book, a cut or color a stylist is proud of, a product that just arrived from a supplier, a holiday or local event coming up. Real events only. If nothing stands out, Claude asks you what is going on this week instead of inventing something.
3. Pick the angle together. Claude suggests one or two angles and says why. You choose, or name your own. Nothing moves forward until you have picked.
4. Draft the posts. Claude writes 2 or 3 posts using templates/promo-post.md, in your voice from business/brand.md. Services and prices come from business/services.md, never from memory or guesswork. A photo from your assets/ folder can go with a post if you point one out.
5. Draft any due-back texts. If regulars in business/clients.md are due for a visit, Claude drafts their texts using templates/sms-reminder.md. Names and preferences come from clients.md only.
6. Approve the batch. Claude lays everything out in one place — every post and every text, together. You go item by item: say yes or no, or tell Claude what to change. Changed items come back for another look. An item you skip is a no. Only items with your fresh yes make the plan.
7. Make the posting plan. Claude ends with a short plan: each approved item, the day and rough time you will post or send it, and where it goes. You do the posting. The plan and the approved drafts are saved to reports/.

## What you get

- 2 or 3 posts and any due-back texts, approved by you and ready to copy.
- A one-page posting plan saved as reports/weekly-marketing-[date].md, with the approved drafts underneath it.

## Never

- Claude never posts or sends anything. Not one word leaves this folder without you.
- Claude never invents an event, a discount, a price, or a happy client. If it is not in your business/ files, your assets/ folder, or something you just said, Claude asks.
- A yes covers that exact item, that week. Last week's yes approves nothing this week, and staying quiet on an item is a no.
- No skin, scalp, or health claims in any post or text. If a draft drifts toward treatment talk, Claude pulls it back to the service and suggests the client see a professional.
- Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
