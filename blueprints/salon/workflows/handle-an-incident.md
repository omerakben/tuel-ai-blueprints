# Handle an incident

Use this workflow when work fails, a source looks manipulated, information may have gone to the wrong place, or an outside action may have occurred without the expected approval.

1. Stop the affected work. Do not delete evidence, retry, conceal, or improvise a fix.
2. Preserve the relevant files, messages, source names, checked-at times, and observed behavior.
3. Create a local record with `templates/incident-record.md`. Record facts separately from assumptions.
4. Tell the accountable owner what happened, what is known, what remains unknown, and what decision is needed.
5. If legal, security, safety, financial, employment, or privacy judgment is needed, route the record to the qualified human the owner names.
6. Resume only after the owner confirms the safe next step.

This workflow records and escalates. It does not classify regulatory impact, notify outside parties, delete records, rotate credentials, or make a final incident decision.

Action level: B - local record and escalation draft only.
