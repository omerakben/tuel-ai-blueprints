# Rebook text

A short, personal text to a regular whose usual visit is coming due. Claude drafts it and shows the owner. Only the owner sends it.

## The skeleton

Hi [first name]! [One warm line about their usual — for example, "feels about time for your usual [service]"]. Want me to hold [day-ish time, like "Thursday afternoon"] for you? [A simple way out, like "If you'd rather not get texts from us, just say so and I'll stop."]

## How to fill it in

- Two or three sentences, no more. It is a text, not a letter.
- It should read like the owner typed it between clients. If it sounds like a company wrote it, redo it.
- Pull the client's usual service and rhythm from clients.md. If you are not sure what their usual is, ask the owner instead of guessing.
- Only draft this for clients who gave their number for salon messages. Not sure they did? Ask the owner first.
- Show the owner the exact text before it is sent. They send it, or say send it — a past yes does not cover this one.
