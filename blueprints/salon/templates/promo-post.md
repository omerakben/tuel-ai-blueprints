# Promo post

A social post for the salon. Claude fills in the skeleton, then shows you the finished post. Nothing goes up until you say yes.

## The skeleton

[Hook line — one short sentence that would make a regular stop scrolling]

[The real reason for this post: an opening / a new product / a stylist's work. One or two plain sentences.]

[One concrete detail — a day, a name from staff.md, a color, a before-and-after from the assets/ folder. Something you could point at.]

[How to book — whatever way clients actually reach this salon, from profile.md or the owner]

[Hashtag row — the salon's usual tags from brand.md, plus one that fits this post; if none are saved yet, ask the owner]

## How to fill it in

- Sound like brand.md. If the post could belong to any salon on the street, rewrite it.
- One post, one message. If there are two things to say, that is two posts.
- Say true things only. If a detail is not in the business/ files, the assets/ folder, or something the owner just said, ask before writing it.
- Mention a price only if the owner gave one for this post. Otherwise leave it out — do not write around it with "great prices".
- Show the owner the finished post before it goes anywhere. Posting is always their move.
