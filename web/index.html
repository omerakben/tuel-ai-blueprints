Straight answers

Before you download

The folder is free. Using Claude is a separate service provided by Anthropic.

Do I need a TUEL account?

No. You can download any blueprint without signing in or sharing an email address with TUEL.

Do I need a Claude account?

Yes. These folders are designed for Cowork in Claude Desktop. Your Claude account and plan are separate from TUEL, and Cowork availability can depend on your plan and computer. Check Anthropic's current Cowork guide.

What happens to my business data?

The download is an ordinary folder you control. When you add files or business details to Claude, Anthropic processes that content under your Claude plan's terms, privacy choices, and retention rules. Consumer and work plans can differ. Review Anthropic's current data-retention guidance before adding sensitive information.

Will Claude send messages, post, book, or pay for me?

No. Every blueprint is draft-first. Claude can prepare an exact item for review, but a named human must freshly approve that exact item and take the real action.

Is this a plugin or an automatic installation?

No. A blueprint is a normal folder of readable files. Downloading it does not install a plugin, connect an account, or grant Claude permission to act.

Can I change the folder?

Yes. The files are yours to read and edit under the MIT license. Keep the human-approval rules intact when you adapt a blueprint.
