# Weekly review

A short look back at the week, so next week starts smarter. Runs well right
after close-the-day on your last working day, or as the opening move of
plan-the-week.

## What it uses

- this week's day notes in reports/
- business/services.md and business/staff.md for context
- templates/weekly-report.md for the shape of the output

## Steps

1. Gather this week's day notes from reports/. If some days are missing, say
   so plainly instead of guessing what happened.
2. Pull out what went well and what dragged, with the evidence from the notes.
3. TODO: add the two or three things a {{BUSINESS_TYPE}} should check every
   week — the patterns or counts that matter most in this line of work.
4. Fill in templates/weekly-report.md as a draft.
5. Show the owner the draft, take corrections, then save the approved version
   as reports/week-report-[date].md.
6. Anything worth remembering long term gets proposed for business/ — the
   owner approves each item before it is written.

Action level: B — drafts only. Nothing is sent, and nothing in business/
changes without the owner's okay.
