# Draft a promo

This playbook writes a small promotion for you to look over — a post for your
page, or a note to bring regulars back in.

## When to run this

A quiet week coming up. A new service to introduce. Stock on the shelf that
needs to move. Any moment you want a little push and would rather not write it
from scratch yourself.

## What Claude reads first

- business/brand.md — how you like to sound
- business/services.md — real service names and real prices
- business/policies.md — rules the promo must not break
- business/clients.md — who might care about this offer

And from you: what you want to promote, and where you plan to share it — on
your social page or as a note to regulars, for example.

## Steps

1. Claude asks what the promo is for and what you want out of it. One question
   at a time, in plain words.
2. Claude checks business/services.md for the real name and the real price of
   whatever you are promoting. If the offer needs a special price or a
   discount, Claude asks you to set it — it never makes up a number.
3. Claude writes up to three versions in your voice, each shaped for where you
   plan to share it.
4. Claude shows you the drafts and asks which one to keep and what to change.
   Rounds of changes are free; take as many as you like.
5. Because a promo is a promise to clients, Claude ends with a plain question:
   happy for this exact wording to go out? You post it or send it yourself,
   from your own accounts, whenever it suits you.
6. Once you pick a winner, Claude saves everything to reports/.

## What you get

Ready-to-use promo text saved as reports/promo-[date].md: the version you chose
on top, the runners-up underneath, and a note of where each was written for.

## Never

- Never post the promo or send it to anyone. Sharing it is yours alone.
- Never invent prices or discounts, and never set an end date the owner did
  not choose.
- Never promise anything that goes against business/policies.md.
- Never make claims about health or skin, and never promise what a treatment
  will do for someone's body. Keep it warm and honest, and leave anything
  clinical out.

Action level: B — Claude drafts; sending, posting, or paying is always the owner's call.
