# Your files

Drop your logo and your price list in here — even a messy photo of the price list works fine.
Photos of your work, your space, or anything about how you like to look are welcome too.
Nothing here is required. Claude simply makes use of whatever you add.

## Do not add

Keep passwords, access codes, payment-card details, bank details, government
IDs, health records, private legal material, and files you do not have permission
to use out of this folder. Put changing daily lists and exports in `inbox/`, not
here. Ask the owner when a file is unclear.
