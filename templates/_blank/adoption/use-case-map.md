# Your AI adoption map

This map helps a {{BUSINESS_TYPE}} add Claude work at a pace the owner can trust. Maturity belongs to each routine, not to the whole business. The owner may stay at any stage with any routine for as long as they want.

No stage authorizes Claude to send, post, pay, book, file, sign, delete, or change an outside system. Use Manual mode for every new or changed routine until the owner has reviewed it again and approved its exact sources, output, and limits.

Only the owner may advance, pause, move back, resume, or retire a routine.
