# Connect your tools (all optional)

Your first win needs no connections at all. Claude works with what is in this
folder and what you tell it — paste from your booking tool or drop an export
into assets/, and that is plenty to start.

Later, if you get tired of copy-pasting, you can connect a tool or two from
inside Claude Desktop. Start with one, keep it read-only, and add it only when
it saves you real time every week.
