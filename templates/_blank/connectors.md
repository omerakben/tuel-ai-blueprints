# Connect your tools (all optional)

Your first win needs no connections at all. Claude works with what is in this
folder and what you tell it. Paste from your booking tool or drop a dated export
into `inbox/`, and that is plenty to start.

Later, if you get tired of copy-pasting, you can connect a tool or two from
inside Claude Desktop. A connected tool may be able to change records as well
as read them. Start in Manual mode, review what the tool can do, and add one
only when it saves you real time every week. This folder still makes drafts;
you perform every outside action.
