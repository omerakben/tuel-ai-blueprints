# Code of conduct

This project serves small-business owners and the people who build for them. Participation here means:

- Be kind and direct. Critique the work, never the person.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks, in any project space.
- Respect owner data absolutely: never commit real customer, staff, or financial information, even in examples or bug reports.
- Assume good faith; resolve disagreements with evidence and patience.

Report problems to the maintainer (see repository owner). Reports are handled promptly and privately. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may ban contributors for behavior that violates this code.
